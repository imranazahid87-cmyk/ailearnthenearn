Advanced Image Prompt Techniques & Templates

Professional AI artists create consistent, realistic and high-quality images by using advanced prompt engineering techniques. In this chapter, you will learn how to control style, composition, lighting and image consistency like a professional.

Character Consistency

When creating multiple images of the same person or character, describe important details consistently.

  • Age
  • Hair Style
  • Face Shape
  • Clothing
  • Accessories
  • Expression
Example Prompt A 28-year-old woman with shoulder-length black hair, blue blazer, white shirt, friendly smile, modern office background, cinematic lighting, ultra realistic, 8K.

Style Consistency

Use the same artistic style throughout a project.

  • Photorealistic
  • Anime
  • Watercolor
  • 3D Render
  • Minimal Illustration
  • Oil Painting

Composition & Camera Control

Camera Angles

Close-up, Eye-Level, Bird's-Eye View, Low Angle, Wide Shot and Portrait.

Lighting Styles

Golden Hour, Studio Lighting, Soft Light, Neon, Cinematic and Backlighting.

Color Palette

Warm, Cool, Pastel, Monochrome, Vibrant or Luxury Black & Gold.

Negative Prompts

Negative prompts tell the AI what should not appear in the image. They help reduce unwanted objects and improve quality.

Example No blur, no watermark, no extra fingers, no text, no low quality, no distortion, no duplicate objects.

Professional Prompt Template

Subject + Style + Environment + Lighting + Camera Angle + Color Palette + Quality + Negative Prompt
