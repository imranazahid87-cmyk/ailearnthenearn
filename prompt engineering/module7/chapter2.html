Realistic, Logo & Product Image Prompts

AI image generators can create professional-quality images for marketing, branding, social media and product advertising. In this chapter, you'll learn how to write prompts for realistic photos, logo concepts and product mockups.

Creating Realistic Images

Realistic images require detailed descriptions. Include the subject, environment, lighting, camera settings and image quality.

Example Prompt A young entrepreneur working on a laptop in a modern office, natural window light, realistic skin texture, shallow depth of field, DSLR photography, 85mm lens, ultra realistic, 8K.

Logo Design Prompts

For logos, describe the brand, style, colors, typography and overall feeling.

Example Prompt Create a modern minimalist logo for an AI education company named "Prompt Master". Use blue and purple colors, clean typography, simple robot icon and transparent background.

Product Advertisement Prompts

AI can generate premium product photos for e-commerce stores and advertisements.

Example Prompt A luxury smartwatch placed on black marble, premium studio lighting, realistic reflections, soft shadows, high-end commercial product photography, ultra detailed, 8K.

Social Media Graphics

Instagram

Create colorful square posts with bold text space and modern design.

YouTube Thumbnail

Use dramatic lighting, bright colors and large empty space for text.

Facebook Banner

Create wide promotional banners with branding and call-to-action areas.

LinkedIn Cover

Design a clean and professional business-themed cover image.

Prompt Formula

Formula

Subject + Style + Background + Lighting + Camera + Colors + Quality + Purpose

Professional Tips

  • Describe the environment clearly.
  • Choose the right lighting style.
  • Mention camera angle and lens if needed.
  • Use high-quality keywords like "Ultra Realistic", "8K", or "Sharp Focus".
  • Keep the prompt focused on one main subject.

Practice Exercise

Write AI image prompts for the following:

  • Luxury perfume advertisement
  • Modern coffee shop logo
  • Gaming laptop product image
  • Instagram promotion for an AI course

Key Takeaways

  • Detailed prompts create better images.
  • Different image types need different prompt styles.
  • Use professional lighting and camera descriptions.
  • Always mention the image purpose.
  • Experiment with prompt variations for better results.
