Module Lessons

Lesson 1: AI Logo Design

A logo is the visual symbol of a brand. A professional logo should be simple, memorable, scalable and suitable for both digital and print media.

Characteristics of a Great Logo

  • Simple & Clean
  • Easy to Remember
  • Scalable
  • Professional Colors
  • Unique Style
  • Balanced Layout
  • Timeless Design

Example Prompt

Create a modern minimalist logo for a premium AI education company, blue and silver color palette, clean geometric icon, professional vector style, white background.

Professional Tips

  • Avoid unnecessary details.
  • Use two or three brand colors.
  • Keep typography clean.
  • Design for multiple sizes.
  • Test the logo on light and dark backgrounds.

Lesson 2: Brand Identity Design

Brand identity includes everything people recognize about a business, including its logo, colors, typography, imagery and overall visual style.

Brand Identity Elements

  • Logo
  • Color Palette
  • Typography
  • Icons
  • Photography Style
  • Social Media Templates
  • Brand Guidelines

Example Prompt

Design a complete visual identity for a luxury coffee brand including logo, packaging colors, typography, business card and social media style guide, premium minimalist design.

Professional Tips

  • Maintain the same visual style.
  • Use consistent fonts.
  • Limit the color palette.
  • Create reusable design templates.
  • Ensure brand consistency across all platforms.

Lesson 3: Social Media Graphics

Social media graphics are one of the most requested AI design services. Businesses need eye-catching visuals for Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, X, Pinterest and YouTube to attract customers and increase engagement.

Popular Social Media Designs

  • Instagram Posts
  • Instagram Stories
  • Facebook Ads
  • LinkedIn Banners
  • YouTube Thumbnails
  • Pinterest Pins
  • X (Twitter) Posts

Example Prompt

Design a modern Instagram post promoting an AI course, blue and purple gradient, clean typography, futuristic elements, professional marketing style, high engagement design, 1080×1080.

Professional Tips

  • Use bold headlines.
  • Keep layouts clean.
  • Include brand colors.
  • Leave space for text.
  • Always include a Call-to-Action.

Lesson 4: Posters & Flyers

Posters and flyers are powerful marketing materials used to promote events, products, businesses and services. A good design quickly captures attention and communicates the main message.

Essential Design Elements

  • Strong Headline
  • High-Quality Images
  • Clear Information
  • Professional Typography
  • Balanced Layout
  • Brand Colors
  • Call-to-Action

Example Prompt

Create a premium business conference poster with a blue and white color scheme, modern typography, abstract technology background, professional corporate style, A3 poster design.

Professional Tips

  • Highlight the main message.
  • Use readable fonts.
  • Maintain visual balance.
  • Avoid overcrowding the design.
  • Export in high resolution for printing.

Lesson 5: Product Packaging Design

Packaging is often the first thing customers notice. AI helps designers quickly create premium packaging concepts for food, cosmetics, electronics and consumer products.

Packaging Components

  • Product Label
  • Logo Placement
  • Color Palette
  • Typography
  • Product Mockup
  • Material Texture
  • Brand Identity

Example Prompt

Luxury organic skincare packaging with matte white and gold finish, elegant typography, premium cosmetic box, realistic product mockup, studio lighting, ultra detailed, 8K.

Professional Tips

  • Match packaging to the target audience.
  • Use premium color combinations.
  • Keep branding consistent.
  • Ensure labels remain readable.
  • Create front, side and back packaging views.

Lesson 6: Advertising Campaign Design

An advertising campaign consists of multiple visuals designed to promote a product or service across different platforms. AI allows designers to generate consistent, high-quality marketing creatives in a fraction of the time.

Campaign Assets

  • Facebook Ads
  • Instagram Posts & Stories
  • Google Display Banners
  • YouTube Thumbnails
  • Email Marketing Graphics
  • Website Hero Banners
  • Promotional Posters

Example Prompt

Create a premium advertising campaign for a luxury smartwatch including Instagram post, Facebook ad, website banner and promotional poster, black and gold branding, realistic product photography, modern typography, ultra detailed, 8K.

Professional Tips

  • Keep branding identical across every design.
  • Use one consistent color palette.
  • Maintain the same typography.
  • Include a strong Call-to-Action.
  • Design for multiple screen sizes.

Apply everything you've learned by creating a complete branding and marketing campaign for a fictional business. Build a professional portfolio project suitable for freelance clients.

Project Tasks
