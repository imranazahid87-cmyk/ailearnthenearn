Module Lessons

Lesson 1: ChatGPT Image Generation

ChatGPT is one of the most powerful AI assistants for image generation. You can describe an idea in natural language, and ChatGPT creates high-quality images based on your instructions.

What Can ChatGPT Create?

  • Realistic Portraits
  • Product Photography
  • Social Media Graphics
  • Logos & Icons
  • Book Covers
  • Posters & Advertisements
  • Concept Art

Example Prompt

Create a luxury perfume bottle on a black marble table with golden lighting, ultra realistic, premium product photography, 8K.

Key Features

  • Easy natural language prompts
  • Professional image quality
  • Image editing support
  • Fast generation
  • Ideal for beginners and professionals

Lesson 2: Google Gemini Image Generation

Google Gemini is an AI platform capable of generating creative images, educational illustrations, marketing graphics and concept artwork using detailed prompts.

Where Gemini Performs Best

  • Educational Content
  • Presentation Graphics
  • Creative Illustrations
  • Business Concepts
  • Storytelling Images

Example Prompt

A modern AI classroom where students learn with holographic screens, bright lighting, futuristic design, ultra detailed, 16:9.

Professional Tip

Write clear prompts that include the subject, background, lighting, camera angle, art style and image quality to get more accurate results.

Lesson 3: Midjourney

Midjourney is one of the world's most popular AI image generation platforms. It is widely used by artists, designers and creative professionals because of its cinematic quality and artistic style.

Best Use Cases

  • Fantasy Art
  • Concept Art
  • Movie Posters
  • Character Design
  • Architecture Visualization
  • Fashion Design

Example Prompt

A futuristic cyberpunk street at night, neon lights, rainy atmosphere, cinematic lighting, ultra realistic, 8K, highly detailed.

Advantages

  • Outstanding artistic quality
  • Excellent lighting and composition
  • Professional concept art
  • Creative visual styles

Limitations

  • Learning curve for beginners
  • May require multiple prompt refinements
  • Some advanced features depend on your subscription plan

Lesson 4: Leonardo AI

Leonardo AI is designed for creators who need high-quality assets for games, marketing, products and digital artwork. It provides excellent control over styles and image generation.

Best Use Cases

  • Game Assets
  • Product Mockups
  • Character Concepts
  • Book Covers
  • Digital Art
  • Business Marketing

Example Prompt

Luxury smartwatch on a reflective black surface, studio lighting, premium product photography, ultra realistic, 8K.

Professional Tips

  • Use detailed prompts for better results.
  • Experiment with different styles.
  • Generate multiple versions before selecting the best one.
  • Save successful prompts for future projects.

Lesson 5: Adobe Firefly

Adobe Firefly is Adobe's AI-powered image generation platform designed for creative professionals. It integrates with Photoshop, Illustrator and Adobe Express, making it ideal for commercial design work.

Best For

  • Marketing Graphics
  • Social Media Designs
  • Advertising Campaigns
  • Product Mockups
  • Commercial Design

Professional Tip

Firefly works best when combined with Adobe Photoshop for advanced editing and professional finishing.

Lesson 6: Ideogram & FLUX

Ideogram is known for generating images with accurate text, while FLUX is recognized for producing highly realistic and detailed AI images.

Ideogram Strengths

  • Text inside images
  • Posters & Banners
  • Logo Concepts
  • Typography Design

FLUX Strengths

  • Ultra Realistic Portraits
  • Product Photography
  • Architecture
  • Professional Commercial Images

Tool Selection Tip

Choose the AI tool according to your project. No single platform is perfect for every creative task.

Quick Comparison
