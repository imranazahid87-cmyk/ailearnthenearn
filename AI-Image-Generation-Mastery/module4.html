Module Lessons

Lesson 1: Introduction to Photography

Photography is the art of capturing light, composition and emotion. Even though AI generates images digitally, understanding photography principles helps you create images that look natural, realistic and professional.

Why Photography Matters in AI

  • Better realism
  • Professional composition
  • Natural lighting
  • Cinematic appearance
  • Commercial-quality visuals

Example Prompt

Portrait of a young entrepreneur in a modern office, natural window light, DSLR photography, ultra realistic, shallow depth of field, 85mm lens, cinematic lighting, 8K.

Professional Tip

Think like a photographer before writing your prompt. Decide what the camera should see, from which angle and under what lighting conditions.

Lesson 2: Camera Angles

Camera angle changes how viewers perceive a subject. Choosing the correct angle can make an image feel powerful, dramatic, natural or cinematic.

Popular Camera Angles

  • Eye Level
  • Low Angle
  • High Angle
  • Bird's Eye View
  • Worm's Eye View
  • Over-the-Shoulder
  • Close-Up
  • Wide Shot

Example Prompt

Luxury sports car photographed from a low angle, sunset lighting, cinematic photography, ultra realistic, 8K, highly detailed.

Professional Tip

Use low-angle shots to make subjects appear stronger and more dramatic. Eye-level shots work best for portraits and realistic scenes.

Lesson 3: Camera Shots

Camera shots determine how much of the subject appears in the frame. Different shots create different emotions and help tell a visual story.

Popular Camera Shots

  • Extreme Close-Up
  • Close-Up
  • Medium Shot
  • Medium Long Shot
  • Full Body Shot
  • Wide Shot
  • Establishing Shot

Example Prompt

A confident businesswoman, medium shot, modern office background, natural daylight, cinematic photography, ultra realistic, 85mm lens, highly detailed, 8K.

Professional Tip

Use close-up shots for emotions and facial expressions. Wide shots are ideal for landscapes, architecture and storytelling scenes.

Lesson 4: Professional Lighting

Lighting is one of the most important elements of photography. It controls mood, depth, realism and visual quality. Even the best prompt can produce poor results if lighting is ignored.

Common Lighting Types

  • Natural Daylight
  • Golden Hour
  • Blue Hour
  • Studio Lighting
  • Soft Lighting
  • Hard Lighting
  • Rim Lighting
  • Back Lighting
  • Volumetric Lighting
  • Cinematic Lighting

Example Prompt

Luxury perfume bottle on black marble, studio lighting, soft shadows, cinematic lighting, premium product photography, ultra realistic, HDR, 8K.

Professional Tips

    Lesson 5: Composition

    Composition is the arrangement of elements inside an image. A strong composition guides the viewer's attention and creates visually balanced, professional-looking AI artwork.

    Essential Composition Rules

    • Rule of Thirds
    • Leading Lines
    • Framing
    • Symmetry
    • Negative Space
    • Depth & Layers
    • Center Composition
    • Balance & Visual Weight

    Example Prompt

    Modern luxury villa surrounded by palm trees, rule of thirds composition, leading lines, sunset lighting, cinematic photography, ultra realistic, HDR, 8K.

    Professional Tips

    • Place the main subject using the Rule of Thirds.
    • Use leading lines to guide the viewer's eyes.
    • Leave negative space for advertisements or text placement.
    • Create depth by including foreground, middle ground and background.
    • Keep the frame clean and avoid unnecessary distractions.

Lesson 6: Camera Lenses

Different camera lenses create different perspectives and visual effects. Choosing the right lens in your prompt makes AI-generated images look much more realistic and professional.

Popular Camera Lenses

  • 24mm – Landscape & Architecture
  • 35mm – Street Photography
  • 50mm – Natural Human Perspective
  • 85mm – Portrait Photography
  • 135mm – Fashion & Telephoto Portraits
  • Macro Lens – Close-up Product Photography
  • Telephoto Lens – Wildlife & Sports

Example Prompt

Beautiful young woman smiling, 85mm lens, shallow depth of field, soft natural light, realistic skin texture, cinematic portrait photography, ultra realistic, 8K.

Professional Tips

  • Use 85mm for realistic portraits.
  • Use 50mm for natural everyday photography.
  • Use 24mm for landscapes and buildings.
  • Macro lenses are perfect for jewelry, watches and food photography.
  • Always combine the lens with lighting and camera angle.

Project Tasks

  • Create a food photography image.

Challenge Prompt

Luxury coffee cup on a wooden table near a large window, golden hour lighting, shallow depth of field, 50mm lens, rule of thirds composition, premium food photography, ultra realistic, HDR, 8K.

Project Goal
