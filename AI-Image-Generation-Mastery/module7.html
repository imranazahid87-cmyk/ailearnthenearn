Module Lessons

Lesson 1: Background Removal

Background removal is one of the most common AI editing tasks. It allows you to isolate the main subject and place it on any background without complex manual editing.

Common Use Cases

  • E-commerce Products
  • Profile Pictures
  • Marketing Graphics
  • Social Media Posts
  • Website Banners
  • Advertising Campaigns

Editing Prompt

Remove the background completely while preserving hair details, clothing edges and natural shadows. Replace it with a clean white studio background.

Professional Tips

  • Preserve hair and transparent objects.
  • Keep realistic edge details.
  • Add natural shadows after replacing the background.
  • Use high-resolution images for better results.

Lesson 2: Inpainting (Object Removal)

Inpainting allows AI to remove unwanted objects and intelligently fill the empty area so the edit looks natural and seamless.

Common Applications

  • Remove People
  • Remove Text
  • Remove Watermarks
  • Remove Power Lines
  • Remove Trash Objects
  • Clean Backgrounds

Editing Prompt

Remove the person standing in the background and naturally reconstruct the park using matching grass, trees and lighting.

Professional Tip

Describe exactly what should replace the removed object instead of only saying "remove it." This produces much more realistic edits.

Lesson 3: Outpainting (Canvas Expansion)

Outpainting allows AI to extend an existing image beyond its original borders. It intelligently creates new content that matches the lighting, perspective, colors and overall style of the original image.

Common Uses

  • Expand Landscape Photos
  • Create Wide Wallpapers
  • Convert Square Images to 16:9
  • Extend Backgrounds
  • Create Social Media Banners
  • Improve Image Composition

Editing Prompt

Expand the image on all four sides while maintaining the same lighting, perspective, mountains, trees and sky. Keep the transition seamless and realistic.

Professional Tips

  • Expand gradually instead of adding huge areas at once.
  • Keep the same lighting direction.
  • Maintain color consistency.
  • Check perspective after every edit.

Lesson 4: Object Replacement

Object replacement lets you swap one object for another while preserving the original lighting, shadows, reflections and composition. This technique is widely used in advertising and product photography.

Common Examples

  • Replace Cars
  • Replace Clothing
  • Change Furniture
  • Swap Products
  • Replace Background Objects
  • Update Marketing Visuals

Editing Prompt

Replace the red sports car with a luxury white electric sedan while preserving the road, reflections, camera angle and sunset lighting.

Professional Tips

  • Match the object's size and perspective.
  • Keep realistic shadows.
  • Adjust reflections if needed.
  • Maintain color harmony throughout the image.

Lesson 5: Image Upscaling & Enhancement

Upscaling increases image resolution while AI enhancement improves sharpness, textures, lighting and overall image quality without significantly reducing realism.

Enhancement Features

  • Increase Resolution
  • Reduce Noise
  • Sharpen Details
  • Improve Skin Texture
  • Restore Fine Details
  • Correct Colors
  • Improve Dynamic Range

Editing Prompt

Upscale this image to 8K resolution while preserving realistic textures, sharpening facial details, reducing noise and maintaining natural colors.

Professional Tips

  • Use the highest quality source image available.
  • Avoid excessive sharpening.
  • Preserve natural skin texture.
  • Always compare the original and enhanced versions.
  • Export images in the appropriate format for web or print.

Lesson 6: Face Restoration & Old Photo Repair

AI can restore damaged photographs by repairing scratches, removing noise, improving facial details, restoring faded colors and increasing image resolution. This technology is widely used to preserve family memories and historical photographs.

What AI Can Restore

  • Old Family Photos
  • Scratches & Dust
  • Blurred Faces
  • Low Resolution Images
  • Faded Colors
  • Torn or Damaged Photos
  • Black & White Images

Editing Prompt

Restore this old family photograph by repairing scratches, enhancing facial details, improving sharpness, restoring natural skin tones and increasing the resolution while preserving the original appearance.

Professional Tips

  • Keep facial features natural.
  • Do not over-smooth skin.
  • Restore colors carefully.
  • Preserve historical details.
  • Always save both the original and restored version.

Lesson 7: Complete AI Editing Workflow Project

Combine everything you've learned to complete a full professional editing project. Edit a realistic AI-generated image from start to finish using multiple AI editing techniques.

Project Tasks

  • Remove the background.
  • Replace unwanted objects.
  • Expand the canvas using Outpainting.
  • Enhance lighting and colors.
  • Upscale the image to 8K quality.
  • Export the final commercial version.

Challenge Prompt

Edit a luxury smartwatch advertisement by replacing the background with a premium black studio setup, improving reflections, enhancing lighting, removing distractions, upscaling to 8K and creating a realistic commercial-quality product image.

Project Goal

Deliver a polished AI-edited image suitable for advertising, e-commerce or social media marketing while maintaining realistic lighting, colors and professional composition.
