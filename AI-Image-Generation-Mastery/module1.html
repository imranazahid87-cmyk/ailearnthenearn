Module Lessons

Lesson 1: Introduction to AI Image Generation

AI Image Generation is a modern technology that creates completely new images from text descriptions called prompts. Instead of drawing manually, users simply describe what they want, and AI produces a unique image within seconds.

What You Will Learn

  • What AI Image Generation is
  • How AI understands text prompts
  • Why this technology is becoming popular
  • Real-world applications

Example Prompt

"A futuristic city at sunset with flying cars, cinematic lighting, ultra realistic, 8K."

Key Takeaways

  • AI creates original images from text.
  • Better prompts produce better results.
  • Every generated image is unique.

Lesson 2: How AI Generates Images

AI models are trained on millions of images and text descriptions. During training, the AI learns patterns, colors, objects, lighting, perspectives, and artistic styles. When you enter a prompt, the AI predicts and creates an entirely new image based on what it has learned.

AI Image Generation Process

  • Step 1: Enter a text prompt.
  • Step 2: AI analyzes the prompt.
  • Step 3: The model generates an image.
  • Step 4: Refine the prompt if needed.

Important Concept

The quality of an AI image depends mainly on the quality and clarity of your prompt.

Lesson 3: Popular AI Image Generation Models

Many companies have developed powerful AI models for generating images. Each model has its own strengths, image quality, speed and creative style.

Most Popular AI Models

  • ChatGPT Images – Excellent for realistic images, illustrations and editing.
  • Google Gemini – Great for creative and educational image generation.
  • Midjourney – Famous for artistic and cinematic images.
  • Leonardo AI – Perfect for game assets and concept art.
  • Adobe Firefly – Designed for professional graphic designers.
  • Ideogram – Best for images containing accurate text.

Important Concept

There is no single "best" AI model. The right tool depends on your goal, such as realistic photography, marketing graphics, concept art, logo design or social media content.

Lesson 4: Image Resolution & Aspect Ratio

Before creating AI images, you must understand image resolution and aspect ratio. These concepts directly affect image quality and where the image can be used.

Image Resolution

Resolution is the number of pixels in an image. Higher resolution means more detail and better print quality.

  • 1024 × 1024 – Square Images
  • 1920 × 1080 – Full HD
  • 2048 × 2048 – High Quality
  • 3840 × 2160 – 4K Resolution

Common Aspect Ratios

  • 1:1 – Instagram Posts
  • 16:9 – YouTube Thumbnails & Videos
  • 9:16 – Shorts & Reels
  • 4:5 – Portrait Posts
  • 3:2 – Photography

Pro Tip

Always choose the correct aspect ratio before generating an image. It reduces cropping and produces better composition.

Lesson 5: Image File Formats

Choosing the correct image format is essential for quality, speed and compatibility. Different formats are designed for different purposes.

PNG

Best for transparent backgrounds, logos and graphics without quality loss.

JPG / JPEG

Ideal for photographs because it keeps file sizes small.

WEBP

Modern format with excellent quality and smaller file size. Perfect for websites.

SVG

Vector format used for icons and logos that stay sharp at any size.

Lesson 6: AI Ethics & Copyright

AI is a powerful creative tool, but it should always be used responsibly and ethically.

  • Respect copyright laws.
  • Avoid creating misleading or harmful content.
  • Use AI to enhance creativity, not replace honesty.
  • Always check commercial usage rights before selling AI-generated work.
  • Give proper credit whenever required by the platform.

Professional Tip

Successful AI creators combine creativity, ethics and technical skills to produce valuable work.
