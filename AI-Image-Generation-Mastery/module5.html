Module Lessons

Lesson 1: Color Theory

Colors influence emotions, attract attention and shape the overall look of an image. Understanding color theory helps you generate AI images that look visually appealing and communicate the right message.

Primary Colors

  • 🔴 Red
  • 🔵 Blue
  • 🟡 Yellow

Popular Color Schemes

  • Monochromatic
  • Complementary
  • Analogous
  • Triadic
  • Split Complementary

Example Prompt

Modern luxury perfume advertisement using a black and gold color palette, premium branding, studio lighting, elegant composition, ultra realistic, 8K.

Professional Tip

Limit your color palette to two or three main colors to maintain visual harmony and avoid a cluttered appearance.

Lesson 2: Visual Hierarchy

Visual hierarchy controls where the viewer looks first. A well-designed image guides attention from the most important element to supporting details.

Ways to Create Hierarchy

  • Large Main Subject
  • Strong Contrast
  • Bright Lighting
  • Leading Lines
  • Negative Space
  • Sharp Focus
  • Strategic Placement

Example Prompt

Luxury smartwatch centered in the frame with a blurred background, dramatic lighting, premium product photography, strong visual hierarchy, ultra realistic, HDR, 8K.

Professional Tip

Always make your main subject the strongest visual element. Avoid adding too many competing objects in one image.

Lesson 3: Typography Basics

Typography is the art of arranging text in a visually appealing way. Even though AI image generation focuses on visuals, understanding typography helps you create posters, banners, social media graphics and advertisements that look professional.

Typography Principles

  • Choose readable fonts.
  • Maintain proper spacing.
  • Use clear text hierarchy.
  • Limit font combinations.
  • Ensure good contrast between text and background.
  • Keep alignment consistent.

Example Prompt

Modern technology event poster with bold professional typography, blue and white color scheme, clean layout, premium branding, ultra realistic.

Professional Tip

When creating marketing visuals, leave enough empty space so headlines and logos can be added without making the design look crowded.

Lesson 4: Balance & Contrast

Balance creates visual stability while contrast highlights the most important elements. Together they make AI-generated images more attractive and easier to understand.

Types of Balance

  • Symmetrical Balance
  • Asymmetrical Balance
  • Radial Balance

Ways to Create Contrast

  • Light vs Dark
  • Large vs Small
  • Warm vs Cool Colors
  • Sharp vs Blurred Background
  • Bold vs Soft Elements

Example Prompt

Minimalist luxury perfume bottle on a black background with golden highlights, dramatic contrast, studio lighting, premium product photography, HDR, ultra realistic, 8K.

Professional Tip

Use contrast to immediately draw attention to the main subject while keeping the rest of the composition clean and balanced.

Lesson 5: Popular Design Styles

Every design style communicates a different mood and purpose. Selecting the right style in your prompt helps AI produce images that match your creative vision.

Popular Design Styles

  • Minimalist
  • Modern
  • Luxury
  • Vintage
  • Retro
  • Cyberpunk
  • Futuristic
  • Corporate
  • Flat Design
  • 3D Illustration

Example Prompt

Luxury cosmetic product displayed in a modern minimalist studio, soft lighting, premium branding, elegant composition, ultra realistic, HDR, 8K.

Professional Tips

  • Choose one main design style.
  • Match colors with the selected style.
  • Keep visual consistency throughout the design.
  • Avoid mixing too many unrelated styles.
  • Study successful advertisements for inspiration.

Lesson 6: Branding & Visual Identity

Branding is more than just a logo. It is the complete visual identity of a business. A strong brand creates trust, recognition and professionalism. AI image generation can help create consistent branding assets quickly and efficiently.

Core Branding Elements

  • Logo Design
  • Brand Colors
  • Typography
  • Icons & Graphics
  • Photography Style
  • Social Media Templates
  • Brand Consistency

Example Prompt

Luxury coffee brand identity, minimalist logo, black and gold color palette, premium packaging mockup, elegant typography, modern branding, studio lighting, ultra realistic, 8K.

Professional Tips

  • Use the same color palette across all designs.
  • Maintain consistent typography.
  • Create a recognizable visual style.
  • Keep branding simple and memorable.
  • Design for both digital and print media.
