Module Lessons

Lesson 1: Introduction to Prompt Engineering

Prompt Engineering is the skill of writing clear and detailed instructions that help AI generate accurate and professional images. A good prompt produces consistent, high-quality results while a weak prompt often creates random or incorrect images.

Why Prompt Engineering Matters

  • Generate professional-quality images
  • Save time by reducing retries
  • Improve creativity and consistency
  • Create commercial-ready artwork
  • Control every detail of the image

Simple Prompt

A cat sitting on a chair.

Professional Prompt

A fluffy white Persian cat sitting on a luxury wooden chair near a large window, soft morning sunlight, realistic fur, cinematic lighting, shallow depth of field, 85mm lens, ultra realistic, 8K.

Lesson 2: Prompt Structure

Professional prompts follow a logical structure. Instead of writing random words, organize your prompt into clear sections.

Professional Prompt Formula

  • Subject
  • Action
  • Environment
  • Lighting
  • Camera Angle
  • Lens
  • Style
  • Image Quality

Example Prompt

A young entrepreneur working on a laptop inside a modern office, large glass windows, warm sunset lighting, eye-level camera angle, 85mm lens, cinematic photography, ultra realistic, highly detailed, 8K.

Professional Tip

Always begin with the main subject, then gradually describe the environment, lighting, camera settings, artistic style and finally the desired image quality.

Lesson 3: Prompt Modifiers & Keywords

Prompt modifiers are powerful keywords that improve image quality, realism, lighting and artistic style. Professional AI creators use these modifiers to achieve consistent and premium results.

Popular Quality Modifiers

  • Ultra Realistic
  • Photorealistic
  • Hyper Detailed
  • HDR
  • 8K Resolution
  • Sharp Focus
  • High Detail

Lighting Modifiers

  • Golden Hour
  • Soft Lighting
  • Cinematic Lighting
  • Studio Lighting
  • Volumetric Lighting
  • Neon Lighting

Style Modifiers

  • Minimalist
  • Luxury
  • Cyberpunk
  • Fantasy
  • Anime Style
  • Oil Painting
  • Watercolor

Example Prompt

Luxury sports car parked in Dubai at sunset, cinematic lighting, ultra realistic, HDR, 8K, highly detailed, sharp focus, premium automotive photography.

Professional Tip

Use only relevant modifiers. Too many unnecessary keywords can reduce image quality instead of improving it.

Lesson 4: Negative Prompts

Negative prompts tell the AI what should NOT appear in the image. They help remove unwanted objects, poor quality and common AI mistakes.

Common Negative Prompts

  • Blurry
  • Low Quality
  • Watermark
  • Text
  • Logo
  • Extra Fingers
  • Extra Arms
  • Duplicate Objects
  • Bad Anatomy
  • Deformed Face
  • Poor Lighting
  • Noise

Example Negative Prompt

blurry, low quality, watermark, text, logo, extra fingers, extra arms, deformed face, duplicate objects, cropped image, bad anatomy

Professional Tip

Always combine a detailed positive prompt with a strong negative prompt to produce cleaner and more professional AI-generated images.

Lesson 5: Advanced Prompt Techniques

Professional AI artists use advanced prompting methods to maintain consistency, improve realism and create commercial-quality images.

Advanced Techniques

  • Character Consistency
  • Reference Images
  • Style References
  • Prompt Weighting
  • Camera Lens Selection
  • Aspect Ratio Control
  • Seed Values (where supported)

Professional Prompt

Confident businesswoman standing inside a luxury office, black business suit, large glass windows, sunset lighting, eye-level shot, 85mm lens, cinematic photography, realistic skin texture, ultra detailed, 8K.

Best Practice

  • Start with the subject.
  • Add environment details.
  • Specify lighting.
  • Mention camera angle.
  • Add lens information.
  • Choose an artistic style.
  • Finish with quality keywords.

Lesson 6: Prompt Optimization & Common Mistakes

Writing prompts is an iterative process. Professional creators rarely use the first prompt as the final version. Instead, they improve and refine prompts until the desired image quality is achieved.

How to Optimize a Prompt

  • Start with a clear subject.
  • Add important details gradually.
  • Describe lighting and environment.
  • Specify camera angle and lens.
  • Use only relevant quality modifiers.
  • Add negative prompts when needed.
  • Review the generated image and refine the prompt.

Common Beginner Mistakes

  • Writing very short prompts.
  • Using too many unrelated keywords.
  • Ignoring lighting and composition.
  • Not using negative prompts.
  • Changing too many variables at once.

Professional Tip

Change only one or two parts of your prompt at a time. This makes it easier to understand what improved or reduced the image quality.

Lesson 2: Camera Angles

Camera angle changes how viewers perceive a subject. Choosing the correct angle can make an image feel powerful, dramatic, natural or cinematic.

Popular Camera Angles

  • Eye Level
  • Low Angle
  • High Angle
  • Bird's Eye View
  • Worm's Eye View
  • Over-the-Shoulder
  • Close-Up
  • Wide Shot

Example Prompt

Luxury sports car photographed from a low angle, sunset lighting, cinematic photography, ultra realistic, 8K, highly detailed.

Professional Tip

Use low-angle shots to make subjects appear stronger and more dramatic. Eye-level shots work best for portraits and realistic scenes.
