01INTRODUCTION

Clipdrop hai kya?

Clipdrop ek image-editing toolkit hai — Photoshop jaisa ek bara software nahi, balke chhotay-chhotay, focused tools ka collection jo ek hi kaam bohat achi tarah karte hain: background hataana, kisi cheez ko mitana, ya lighting badalna.

  • Har tool alag purpose ke liye — seekhna asaan hai
  • Fast results — kaam seconds mein ho jaata hai
  • Web, app aur API — teeno tareeqon se available
02TOOL

Remove Background — ek click mein

Kisi bhi image ka background instantly hata dena — bilkul saaf, precise edges ke saath. Product photos, profile pictures ya design assets ke liye khaas taur par useful.

03TOOL

Cleanup — object removal

Image mein kisi unwanted cheez par brush karo — chahe wo koi insaan ho, koi tar ho, ya koi daag — aur Clipdrop usay naturally hata kar background fill kar deta hai.

Use Case Tourist photo se background mein khare ajnabi logon ko hataana
04TOOL

Uncrop — frame badhaana

Chhoti ya crop hui image ko kisi bhi direction mein expand karo — AI baaki scene ko khud generate kar deta hai, jaise original photo kabhi bari thi hi.

08GUIDELINES
