01Introduction

Ideogram hai kya?

Ideogram ek AI image tool hai jo apni text-rendering accuracy ke liye jaana jaata hai — yani ye tasveer ke andar spelling-perfect words, logos aur posters bana sakta hai, jo zyadatar AI tools ke liye mushkil hota hai.

  • Sabse alag feature — clean, readable text directly image mein
  • Design-focused — logos, posters, banners ke liye behtareen
  • Fast aur free tier — beginners ke liye asaan shuruat
02Signature Feature

Text-in-Image ka jaadu

Jahan doosre tools text ko ghalat ya toota-phoota likhte hain, wahan Ideogram lafzon ko saaf aur sahi tareeqe se render karta hai. Isi liye ye logo aur poster design ke liye sabse pehla intekhab hai.

Example Prompt A vintage travel poster with the text "VISIT THE MOUNTAINS" in bold retro lettering, sunset colors

08Responsible Use

Zimmedari se istemal

Yaad Rahe

Kisi maujooda brand ka logo ya trademark hu-bahu copy karna, ya kisi doosre business ka naam istemal karke design banana — dono se bachna zaroori hai. Ideogram ko naye, apne khud ke ideas banane ke liye istemal karein.
