Module 01

DALL·E 3 hai kya?

DALL·E 3, OpenAI ka text-to-image model hai jo ChatGPT ke andar seedha kaam karta hai. Iska naam surrealist painter Salvador Dalí aur Pixar ke robot WALL·E se mila kar rakha gaya hai — art aur technology ka mel.

  • ChatGPT ke andar — alag app khulne ki zaroorat nahi
  • Conversational editing — baat karke image ko badalna
