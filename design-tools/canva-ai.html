Foundation · Lesson 1 5 min read

Canva Kya Hai?

Duniya ka sabse popular free design tool — bilkul drag & drop, koi coding nahi, koi design degree nahi chahiye.

Canva ek browser-based graphic design platform hai jo 2013 mein launch hua. Aaj 190+ countries mein 150 million se zyada log isko use karte hain. Social media posts, presentations, logos, CVs, posters — sab kuch ek hi jagah bana sakte ho.

💡
Simple baat: Photoshop ya Illustrator seekhne mein months lagte hain. Canva mein aap pehle din hi professional-looking design bana sakte ho.

Canva Kyun Use Karein?

🆓
Free Plan Powerful Hai
250,000+ templates, 1M+ elements — free mein. Pro plan zaruri nahi shuru mein.
🌐
Browser Mein Kaam Karo
Koi software download nahi, koi heavy PC nahi chahiye. Phone pe bhi kaam karta hai.
🤝
Team Collaboration
Team ke saath ek saath ek design pe kaam karo — Google Docs jaisa.
📤
Easy Export
PNG, JPG, PDF, MP4, GIF — jis format mein chahiye export karo instantly.

Canva vs Photoshop — Kya Farq Hai?

FeatureCanvaPhotoshop
Seekhne mein time1-2 dinMahine
CostFree (Pro: $15/mo)$57/mo
Templates250,000+Limited
Advanced editingBasicProfessional
Social media usePerfectPossible
🎯
Konse log Canva use karte hain? Bloggers, small businesses, teachers, students, social media managers, NGOs, freelancers — basically jise bhi visuals chahiye.

Canva Editor Ka Tour

Editor ke har button ko jaano — phir koi confusion nahi hogi.

Jab aap koi template open karte ho ya new design start karte ho, toh Canva Editor khuljata hai. Iska interface 5 main parts mein divided hai:

Canva Editor Layout
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ [File] [Resize] [Undo/Redo] [Share] [Download] │ ← Top Bar
├──────────┬──────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Elements │ │
│ Photos │ CANVAS (Design Area) │
│ Text │ │
│ Background│ [Your Design Here] │
│ Uploads │ │
│ Apps │ │
├──────────┴──────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Page 1 + (Add page) │ ← Bottom Bar
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

Har Part Kya Karta Hai?

📌
Left Panel (Toolkit)
Templates, Elements, Photos, Text, Background, Uploads, Apps — yahan sab milta hai
🖼️
Canvas (Center)
Yahan aap click, drag, drop, resize karte ho — yeh aapka actual design area hai
⚙️
Top Toolbar
Selected element ki properties — font, color, size, alignment, effects sab yahan aate hain
📄
Bottom Bar
Multiple pages add karo — presentations ya multi-page documents ke liye
🖱️
Most Important Clicks: Kisi bhi element pe click karo = select. Double click = edit. Right click = options menu. Khali jagah click = deselect.
Designing · Lesson 4 6 min · Hands-on

Apna Pehla Design Banao

Step by step — ek Instagram post abhi banate hain. Sath follow karo!

Hum ek Instagram Post banayenge. Canva mein yeh sabse zyada use hone wala design type hai.

Step 1 — Template Choose Karo

  • 1
    Dashboard pe search bar mein "Instagram Post" type karo
  • 2
    Hazaron templates dikhenge — koi bhi click karo jo aapko pasand aaye
  • 3
    "Customize this template" pe click karo

Step 2 — Text Edit Karo

  • 1
    Text pe double click karo — editing mode mein aa jayega
  • 2
    Purana text delete karo, apna text likhlo
  • 3
    Top toolbar se font, size, color change karo

Step 3 — Background Change Karo

Background Change Process
Background click karo → Top pe "Background color" ya "Image" option aayega

Option A: Solid color — color picker se apni marzi ka color chunno
Option B: Gradient — do colors ka blend
Option C: Image — Left panel se Photos section mein se koi bhi photo drag karo

Step 4 — Export Karo

  • 1
    Top right mein "Share" button click karo
  • 2
    "Download" select karo
  • 3
    Format chunno: PNG (social media ke liye best) ya PDF (print ke liye)
  • 4
    "Download" pe click — aapka design ready!
🏆
Congratulations! Aapne apna pehla professional design bana liya. Ab yahi process har design type mein repeat hoti hai — sirf template alag hoti hai.
Designing · Lesson 5 5 min

Typography & Colors Ka Magic

Ek achha design aur ek professional design mein yahi farq hota hai — font aur color choices.

Typography — Fonts Kaise Chunein

Canva mein 1000+ Google Fonts free hain. Lekin sab fonts ek saath use karna galat hai!

✍️
Golden Rule: Ek design mein maximum 2 fonts use karo.
Heading font: Bold, decorative (attention grab kare)
Body font: Clean, readable (easy to read)

Font Pairing Examples

🎨
Playfair + Lato
Elegant, magazine-style — blogs, boutiques, portfolios ke liye perfect
💼
Montserrat + Open Sans
Modern, corporate — business presentations aur formal content
🎉
Lobster + Raleway
Fun, creative — social media, events, parties ke liye
📚
Merriweather + Source Sans
Classic, readable — long-form content, educational material

Color Theory — Simple Version

Canva Color Palette Tool
60-30-10 Rule follow karo:

🟣 60% — Dominant color (background, main areas)
🔵 30% — Secondary color (cards, sections)
🟡 10% — Accent color (buttons, highlights)

Canva mein: Left Panel → Elements → Color Palettes → Brand palettes ya auto-generate feature use karo
🌈
Pro Tip: Coolors.co ya Adobe Color se palette generate karo, phir woh hex codes Canva mein paste karo. Instant professional look!

Designing · Lesson 6 5 min

Elements, Photos & Graphics

Canva ka asli power yahan hai — millions of free assets ek click door.

Elements Panel Kya Hai?

Left sidebar mein "Elements" click karo — yahan aapko milega:

Lines & Shapes Stickers & GIFs Icons & Illustrations Frames (Photo holders) Charts & Graphs Grids & Mockups

Photos Use Karne Ke 3 Tarike

  • A
    Canva Photos: Left panel → Photos → search karo (100M+ free Pexels/Pixabay photos included)
  • B
    Apni Photos Upload: Left panel → Uploads → "Upload files" — JPG, PNG, SVG, MP4 sab chalega
  • C
    Frames Use Karo: Elements → Frames — photo ko ek shape ke andar fit karo automatically
Photo Editing Options (Click karne par milta hai)
📸 Edit Image — Brightness, contrast, saturation adjust karo
✂️ Crop — Photo trim karo
🎨 Filters — Instagram-style filters apply karo
🔧 Effects — Blur, shadow, duotone
🗑️ Background Remover — Pro feature (background hata do!)
🔄 Flip — Mirror karo horizontal ya vertical

Design Tricks Jo Sabse Zyada Kaam Aate Hain

🔒
Lock Elements
Right click → Lock. Important elements accidentally move na hon.
📋
Copy Style
Element select → "Copy style" — same formatting doosre element pe apply karo
📐
Alignment Tools
Multiple elements select → Arrange → Align. Sab perfectly center mein!
📚
Layers Panel
File → Layers — elements ka order control karo. Koi cheez chupp rahi hai? Yahan dekho.

Canva Se Paise Kamao

Sirf hobby nahi — Canva skills se real income possible hai. Yahan 6 proven tarike hain.

📦
1. Canva Templates Sell Karo
Canva Creator Program join karo — apni templates Canva marketplace pe sell karo. Top creators $1000+/month earn karte hain.
🛍️
2. Etsy Pe Templates
Resume, wedding card, Instagram pack templates banao aur Etsy pe $5–$30 mein sell karo. Passive income!
💼
3. Freelance Design
Fiverr ya Upwork pe social media management, logo design, presentation design offer karo. $100–$500/project.
📱
4. Social Media Manager
Local businesses ke social media handle karo — monthly retainer $150–$500. Sirf Canva chahiye!
📚
5. Online Course ya Tutorials
Canva seekhao YouTube pe ya Udemy pe — channel monetize karo ya course sell karo.
🖨️
6. Print on Demand
Redbubble, Zazzle pe designs upload karo — T-shirts, mugs, posters pe print hokr sell hote hain automatically.
