Module 01

Adobe Firefly hai kya?

Adobe Firefly Adobe ka generative AI tool hai jo sirf text likh kar images, art, effects aur designs bana deta hai. Iska naam "firefly" isi liye rakha gaya — jaise jugnu andhere mein roshni paida karta hai, waise hi ye tool sirf alfaaz se visuals paida karta hai.

  • Text-to-Image — likho, aur tasveer ban jaye
  • Commercially safe — Adobe Stock aur licensed data par trained, isliye business mein use karna safe hai
  • Adobe apps ke andar integrated — Photoshop, Illustrator, Express mein bhi maujood
Module 02

Text-to-Image ka jaadu

Ye Firefly ka sabse popular feature hai. Aap ek jumla likhte hain, aur AI 4 alag versions bana kar deta hai. Aap style, lighting, color aur mood — sab control kar sakte hain.

Example Prompt "Ek chhota sa gaon, subah ki dhundh mein, warm golden light, watercolor painting style"
Module 03

Generative Fill — editing ka superpower

Kisi bhi tasveer ka hissa select karo aur bata do kya chahiye — Firefly us jagah ko naturally fill kar deta hai. Chahe kuch hataana ho, badalna ho, ya naya add karna ho.

Module 04

Text Effects aur stylish typography

Sirf ek lafz likh kar usay fire, ice, galaxy, ya metal jaisi styles mein badal dena — bina kisi design skill ke. Ye feature social media posts aur thumbnails ke liye bohat useful hai.

Module 05

Behtareen Prompt likhne ka tareeqa

Achi image ka raaz achay prompt mein hota hai. Ye formula yaad rakhein:

Formula Subject + Setting + Lighting + Style + Mood
  • Specific raho — "cat" ki bajaye "orange tabby cat sitting on a windowsill"
  • Lighting batao — golden hour, neon lights, soft daylight
  • Style zaroor likho — 3D render, oil painting, cinematic photo
