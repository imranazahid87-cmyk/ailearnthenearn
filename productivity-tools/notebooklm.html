01

NotebookLM kya hai?

FOUNDATION

NotebookLM Google ka AI-powered research aur note-taking tool hai — yeh Gemini se powered hai aur aapke apne documents ko padh kar unhi se jawab deta hai. Matlab jab aap koi sawaal poochte ho, toh yeh apni training data se nahi — sirf aapke upload kiye hue sources se jawab deta hai.

Isi wajah se yeh normal chatbots se zyada trustworthy hai — research, study, aur legal review jaise kaamon ke liye, jahan galat jawab mehenga pad sakta hai.

Yaad rakho: NotebookLM internet se jawab nahi deta — sirf woh sources jo aap khud upload karte ho.
02

Notebook banana aur sources upload karna

GETTING STARTED

notebooklm.google par jao, apne Google account se sign in karo, aur "Create" par click karke ek nayi notebook banao. Aapko teen panels dikhenge: left mein sources, center mein chat, aur right mein Studio.

  • Ek notebook mein 50 tak sources upload kar sakte ho — PDFs, Google Docs, Slides, websites, YouTube video transcripts, audio files, aur Google Sheets.
  • Handwritten notes, whiteboard photos, ya screenshots bhi upload karke unhe analyze karwa sakte ho.
  • Notebooks ko teammates ke saath share bhi kar sakte ho — group research projects ke liye.
03

Citations ke saath sawaal-jawab

CORE SKILL

Ek baar sources upload hone ke baad, chat panel mein seedha sawaal poochna shuru karo. Har jawab ke saath ek chhota numbered citation[1] milta hai — click karte hi woh seedha us original document ki us line par le jaata hai jahan se jawab aaya.

Yeh feature khaas taur par tab kaam aata hai jab aapke paas multiple conflicting sources hon — aap seedha pooch sakte ho "yeh sources kahan disagree karte hain?" aur NotebookLM exact lines quote karke dikha dega jahan farq hai.

04

Audio Overview — apne notes ko podcast banao

VIRAL FEATURE

Yeh wahi feature hai jisne NotebookLM ko popular banaya. Ek click mein aapke sources se ek podcast-style audio ban jaata hai, aur ab yeh 4 formats mein aata hai:

  • Deep Dive — default two-host conversation.
  • Brief — ek hi speaker, 2 minute ke andar key points.
  • Critique — do hosts aapke kaam (essay, design doc) ko constructively evaluate karte hain.
  • Debate — topic par formal back-and-forth.

80+ languages support hoti hain, aur English mein length (Shorter/Default/Longer) bhi adjust kar sakte ho, saath hi ek prompt de kar hosts ko guide kar sakte ho ki kis cheez par focus karein.

05

Video Overview — 2026 ka naya feature

NEW

Sirf audio nahi — ab NotebookLM ek narrated, animated deep-dive video bhi banata hai aapke sources se. Format customize kar sakte ho (explainer ya brief), language choose kar sakte ho, aur visual style bhi — jaise whiteboard, kawaii, watercolor, ya classic.

Tip: "What should the AI hosts focus on?" wale prompt box mein apna context daalo — NotebookLM khud bhi suggestions deta hai based on aapke sources.
06

Mind Map aur Reports

VISUALIZE

Interactive Mind Map feature aapke sources se ek visual diagram banata hai jo high-level ideas ko connect karke dikhata hai. Agar aapke paas 50 documents hain, toh yeh web-jaisa view overarching concepts pakadne mein madad karta hai jo sirf text summary mein miss ho sakte hain.

Studio panel se aap alag-alag reports bhi generate kar sakte ho — jaise briefing doc, study guide, aur FAQ.

07

Presentations directly notebook ke andar

2026 UPDATE

Ab NotebookLM presentations bhi bana sakta hai aapke sources se — aur naye in-app editing feature ke saath, aap text, images, aur slides ko real-time edit kar sakte ho, bina export ya convert kiye.

08

Beginner ke liye pro tips

SHORTCUTS
  • Shuruaat mein simple sawaal poocho — pehle samjho NotebookLM aapke sources ko kaise interpret karta hai.
  • Meeting notes ya research papers upload karke Audio Overview banao — commute ya chores ke waqt "padhne" ke bajaye "sunna" easy ho jaata hai.
  • Apne work journals aur project notes upload karke pooch sakte ho: "mere top 3 impact areas kya hain, evidence ke saath" — yeh appraisal likhne mein kaafi help karta hai.
  • Free version kaafi robust hai — paid version zyada notebooks banane aur access badhane ke liye hai.
