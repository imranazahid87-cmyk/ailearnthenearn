Shuruaat

NotebookLM hai kya cheez?

Normal AI chatbots kabhi-kabhi galat cheez bana dete hain (hallucinate karte hain) kyunki wo apni training se jawab dete hain. NotebookLM alag hai — ye sirf aapke diye gaye documents ko "source of truth" maan kar jawab deta hai, aur har fact ke saath uska exact source citation deta hai.

Source-Grounded

Jawab sirf aapke uploaded material se aate hain — outside knowledge mix nahi hoti.

Inline Citations

Har jawab mein click karne layak citation hoti hai jo seedha source ke us hisse par le jaati hai.

Audio Overview

Apne documents ko ek podcast-style conversation mein convert kar sakte ho, sunne ke liye.

Core Features

NotebookLM ke andar kya-kya milta hai

Poocho: "Sabhi meeting notes se action items list karo"

Sirf upload kiye gaye notes se hi answer aata hai, har point ke saath source citation ke.

One-click: "Audio Overview generate karo"

Do AI hosts aapke documents par ek natural conversation-style discussion karte hain — sunte-sunte revise ho jaata hai.

Poocho: "Is chapter ka study guide aur quiz banao"

Flashcards, summary aur practice questions apne materials se hi generate ho jaate hain.

Hands-On

Kaise use karein

  1. Naya notebook banao

    Ek topic ke liye ek notebook — jaise "Thesis Research" ya "Client X Notes".

  2. Sources upload karo

    PDFs, Google Docs, websites, YouTube links, ya paste kiya hua text — sab chalta hai.

  3. Sawaal poocho

    Apne documents ke baare mein natural language mein questions poochho.

  4. Citation verify karo

    Har jawab ke saath diya gaya source click karke original line dekh lo.

  5. Audio ya notes generate karo

    Zaroorat ho toh audio overview ya study guide bhi bana lo.

Zaroori

Do's aur Don'ts

Kya NotebookLM internet se bhi jawab deta hai? ▾
Nahi, by default ye sirf aapke uploaded sources tak limited rehta hai — yahi iski sabse badi strength hai.
Kya citations hamesha verify karni chahiye? ▾
Haan, especially important decisions ke liye — citation click karke original source line zaroor dekh lo.
Kitne sources ek notebook mein daal sakte hain? ▾
Ek achhi khaasi limit hoti hai (jaise 50 sources tak), jo alag-alag account type ke hisaab se vary karti hai.
