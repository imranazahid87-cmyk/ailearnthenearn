Shuruaat

Gamma hai kya cheez?

Sochiye ek blank slide banane ki jagah aap sirf apna idea type karte ho, aur Gamma poore content ko cards mein todkar, har card ko design karke, relevant images tak daal deta hai. Ye ek "card-based" format use karta hai jo slides, docs, aur webpages — teeno ban sakta hai.

One-Prompt Generation

Ek topic ya outline dijiye, poora structured content mil jaata hai — text, layout, images sab.

Card-Based Format

Har section ek "card" hai jo independently editable hai — flexible aur modular.

Multi-Format Export

Same content ko presentation, doc, ya standalone webpage ki tarah publish kar sakte ho.

Hands-On

Content banane ka tarika

  1. Topic ya paste karo

    Ek line ka idea likho, ya apna existing rough text paste kar do.

  2. Outline review karo

    Gamma pehle ek section-wise outline dikhata hai — yahin edit kar sakte ho generate se pehle.

  3. Theme chuno

    Ready themes mein se pick karo, ya apna brand color/font set karo.

  4. Generate karo

    AI poora content, layout aur images ke saath cards bana deta hai.

  5. Refine aur publish karo

    Individual cards edit karo, phir presentation/doc/web format mein publish ya share karo.

Do's aur Don'ts

Kya generated content ko fact-check karna chahiye? ▾
Haan, AI-generated text mein galat facts aa sakte hain — publish karne se pehle content verify zaroor karo.
Kya Gamma PowerPoint ki jagah use kar sakte hain? ▾
Casual aur fast decks ke liye haan, lekin bahut complex custom animations ya corporate templates ke liye traditional tools better ho sakte hain.
Kya main free mein use kar sakta hoon? ▾
Haan, ek free plan hai limited AI credits ke saath; zyada generations ke liye paid plan chahiye.
