Shuruaat

Zapier AI hai kya cheez?

Zapier ek "trigger → action" automation platform hai — jab ek app mein kuch hota hai (trigger), toh dusre app mein khud kuch ho jaata hai (action). Pehle in "Zaps" ko manually banane ke liye har step choose karna padta tha. Ab AI ki madad se aap sirf plain English/Hinglish mein bata sakte ho ki kya automate karna hai, aur AI khud steps bana deta hai.

Core Features

Roz kaam aane wale features

Batao: "Jab Google Form submit ho, tab ek welcome email bhejo"

AI khud pehchan leta hai ki trigger Google Forms hai aur action Gmail — poora Zap draft ban jaata hai.

Zap ke andar: "Is customer message ka sentiment (positive/negative) batao"

AI step us data ko analyze karke agla action decide karne mein madad karta hai — jaise negative ho toh manager ko alert.

Bana lo: Ek website chatbot jo apne data se customer sawaalon ka jawab de

Zapier se ek custom AI chatbot banake website par embed kar sakte hain, koi coding zaroori nahi.

Hands-On

Ek Zap banane ka tarika

  1. Trigger app chuno

    "Kab automation shuru ho" — jaise naya email, form submission, ya naya row.

  2. Action app chuno

    "Kya karna hai" — jaise Slack message bhejna ya Sheet mein data add karna.

  3. AI se describe karo (optional)

    Manually steps chunne ke bajaye seedha plain language mein poora flow bata do.

  4. Test karo

    Zap ko ek sample data ke saath test run karo, output verify karo.

  5. On karo

    Ek baar satisfied ho jao, Zap ko "on" kar do — ab ye background mein khud chalta rahega.

Zaroori

Do's aur Don'ts

Kya har Zap ko test karna chahiye? ▾
Haan, "on" karne se pehle hamesha ek test run karo — galat trigger ya action se duplicate ya galat data ban sakta hai.
Kya sensitive data (customer info) automation mein safe hai? ▾
Zapier data securely handle karta hai, lekin phir bhi kaunsa data kis app tak jaa raha hai ye zaroor check karo, especially compliance-heavy industries mein.
Kya free plan mein sab Zaps chalte hain? ▾
Free plan mein limited tasks/month milte hain aur multi-step Zaps ke liye paid plan chahiye hota hai.
