Shuruaat

Copilot hai kya cheez?

Microsoft 365 Copilot ek AI hai jo seedha aapke Office apps ke andar kaam karta hai — koi alag se tab ya website nahi. Ye aapki files, emails aur meetings ka context samajhta hai (agar company ne access diya ho), aur us context ke saath jawab deta hai.

Context-aware

Aapke document, spreadsheet ya inbox ka data padh kar jawab deta hai — generic AI jaisa nahi.

Har app mein

Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, Teams — sabme same Copilot, alag-alag kaam ke liye.

Chat + Sidebar

Ek chat window side mein khulti hai jahan aap normal Hinglish/English mein instruction dete ho.

Lambi email chain padhne ki zaroorat nahi, seedha decisions aur action items mil jaate hain.

Prompt: "Aaj ki meeting mein mujhe assign hue action items list karo"

Live transcript se aapke naam wale action points nikal kar deta hai — meeting miss ho jaaye tab bhi kaam nahi rukta.

Skill

Achha prompt kaise likhein

  1. Role aur goal batao

    "Ek HR manager ki tarah likho" — Copilot ko pata chalta hai kis tone mein jawab dena hai.

  2. Source specify karo

    "Is attached PDF se" ya "is sheet ke column C se" — bina batayenge Copilot guess karega.

  3. Format maango

    "Bullet points mein", "table banao", "3 lines mein" — output shape define karta hai.

  4. Refine karo, restart mat karo

    Pehla jawab perfect nahi? "Isse thoda short karo" bolo — Copilot pichle jawab ko hi edit karega.

Zaroori

Do's aur Don'ts

Kya Copilot confidential company data safe rakhta hai? ▾
Haan, Microsoft 365 Copilot aapke organization ke data ko training ke liye use nahi karta — lekin phir bhi sensitive data share karte waqt company ki policy follow karo.
Kya har output ko blindly trust karna chahiye? ▾
Nahi. Numbers, facts aur quotes hamesha manually verify karo — especially financial ya legal documents mein.
Kya Copilot ke liye alag subscription chahiye? ▾
Haan, ye Microsoft 365 ke normal plans se alag ek add-on license hai jo organization enable karti hai.
