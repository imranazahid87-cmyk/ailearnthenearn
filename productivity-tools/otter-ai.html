Otter AI kya hai?

FOUNDATION

Otter AI ek meeting transcription tool hai jo audio ko real-time text mein badalta hai. Yeh 10 million+ users ke saath sabse recognized AI transcription names mein se ek hai.

Sabse bada use case: meeting ke dauraan note-taking se free ho jana — aap conversation par focus karo, Otter poori transcript, summary, aur action items sambhal lega.

Meeting join karna aur record karna

GETTING STARTED

Otter apna calendar (Google Calendar, Microsoft Outlook, iOS Calendar) connect karta hai aur scheduled meetings mein OtterPilot ke naam se ek virtual participant ki tarah khud join ho jaata hai — Zoom, Microsoft Teams, ya Google Meet, teeno par.

Transparency: OtterPilot ek visible bot ki tarah call mein dikhta hai — sabhi participants ko pata chalega ki recording ho rahi hai.

Live transcript aur captions

CORE SKILL

Meeting ke dauraan audio real-time text mein convert hota hai, aur transcript sabhi participants ke saath share hoti hai. Har line timestamped hoti hai, taaki baad mein specific moment par jump kar sako, aur speaker identification se pata chalta hai kisne kya bola.

Aap live transcript mein important parts highlight bhi kar sakte ho jab woh bol rahe ho — taaki baad mein poori transcript dobara padhne ki zaroorat na pade.

Automatic summary aur action items

POST-MEETING

Meeting khatam hone ke kuch minute baad, Otter NLP use karke ek summary generate karta hai jisme key points, decisions, aur action items highlight hote hain — poori transcript padhe bina hi overview mil jaata hai.

Aap takeaways ko highlight kar ke teammates ko tag bhi kar sakte ho, taaki action items directly unhe assign ho jayein.

Otter AI Chat se sawaal poochna

Q&A

Otter ek built-in AI chatbot bhi deta hai jo meeting content ke baare mein sawaalon ka jawab de sakta hai — jaise "Q3 launch ke baare mein kya decide hua?" — bina poori transcript scroll kiye.

Kuch bhi transcribe karo

FLEXIBILITY

Sirf live meetings hi nahi — Otter kisi bhi YouTube video ko transcribe kar sakta hai (video play karo, phir Otter mein Record dabao), aur Dropbox mein audio/video file daalte hi woh automatically transcribe ho jaati hai.

In-person conversations bhi record kar sakte ho apne phone se — desktop app, iOS, Android, aur Chrome extension, sab available hain.

Search aur centralized repository

ORGANIZE

Har meeting note ek searchable, centralized repository mein store hoti hai. Folders bana sakte ho, comments add kar sakte ho, aur team-wide access ke liye Single Sign-On (SSO) aur usage analytics jaise admin controls bhi hain.

Pro tips aur honest limitations

HONEST NOTES
  • Transcription accuracy typically 85-90% hoti hai — background noise ya heavy accents ke saath thoda kam ho sakti hai.
  • Insights (summary, action items) meeting khatam hone ke baad hi milte hain — live call ke dauraan real-time AI help ke liye yeh best fit nahi hai.
  • Free plan mein 300 minutes/month milte hain — usse zyada chahiye toh paid plan dekhna hoga.
Yaad rakho: Sensitive ya confidential meetings record karne se pehle sabhi participants ko batana zaroori aur ethical hai — kai jagah legally bhi.
