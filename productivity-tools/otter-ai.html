Shuruaat

Otter AI hai kya cheez?

Otter Zoom, Google Meet ya Teams meetings mein join hokar (ya phone se live record karke) speech-to-text transcript banata hai. Ye alag-alag speakers ko pehchanta hai, key points highlight karta hai, aur meeting khatam hote hi ek summary + action items ready kar deta hai.

Live Transcription

Meeting chalte hi real-time mein text ban raha hota hai — recording khatam hone ka wait nahi karna padta.

Speaker Identification

Har line ke saath pata chalta hai kis speaker ne kya bola — mixed-voice recording bhi samjha deta hai.

Auto Action Items

Meeting mein bole gaye commitments ko khud detect karke ek checklist bana deta hai.

Core Features

Roz kaam aane wale features

Meeting join karo: Otter Zoom link ke saath calendar se auto-join ho jaata hai

Aapko manually record button dabane ki zaroorat nahi — calendar sync se ye khud aa jaata hai.

Poocho: "Budget approval ke baare mein kya decide hua tha?"

Poore transcript mein se relevant hissa dhoondh kar seedha jawab deta hai, timestamp ke saath.

Meeting khatam hote hi: Auto-generated summary + action items + key decisions

Bina kuch type kiye, meeting ke turant baad ek clean summary sabko email ho sakta hai.

Hands-On

Otter use karne ka tarika

  1. Calendar connect karo

    Google/Outlook calendar se link karo taaki Otter apne aap upcoming meetings mein join ho sake.

  2. Meeting record hone do

    Live transcript aapko khud dikhta hai, bina wait kiye — screen share bhi capture hota hai.

  3. Highlights maaro

    Meeting ke dauraan important lines ko star/highlight karo taaki baad mein jaldi mile.

  4. Summary check karo

    Meeting khatam hote hi AI summary aur action items generate ho jaate hain — review karo.

  5. Share ya export karo

    Team ko transcript ya summary email/Slack se share karo, ya doc mein export karo.

Zaroori

Do's aur Don'ts

Kya sabko batana zaroori hai ki meeting record ho rahi hai? ▾
Haan, recording consent bahut jagah legally zaroori hota hai — meeting shuru hone se pehle sabko inform karo.
Kya transcript 100% accurate hota hai? ▾
Nahi, accents, background noise ya technical terms ki wajah se kabhi-kabhi galtiyan aa sakti hain — important parts verify kar lo.
Kya sensitive meetings record karni chahiye? ▾
Confidential ya HR-sensitive discussions ke liye company policy zaroor check karo before recording.
