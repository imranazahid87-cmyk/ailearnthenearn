🤖 What is Grok AI?

Grok AI is an advanced artificial intelligence assistant developed by xAI. It is designed to provide fast, intelligent and conversational responses. Grok is especially known for its real-time information, creative thinking and coding abilities. It can answer questions, explain concepts, generate ideas and assist with many everyday tasks.
