Lesson 3

🔎 Smart Search & Research with Perplexity AI

Perplexity AI is much more than a chatbot. It is an AI-powered research assistant that searches trusted websites, understands your question and provides a complete answer with source references. This saves time and helps you find reliable information quickly.

🔍 Basic Search

Start by typing your question naturally. Perplexity understands complete sentences better than short keywords.

Example:
  • ❌ SEO
  • ✅ Explain SEO for beginners with practical examples.
  • ❌ AI
  • ✅ What is Artificial Intelligence and how is it used in daily life?

📚 Research Like a Professional

For research, ask detailed questions instead of simple ones.

Example Prompts:
  • Compare ChatGPT and Gemini.
  • Best freelancing skills for beginners.
  • How to start a digital marketing agency?
  • Latest AI tools for students.
  • Top SEO strategies with examples.

💬 Ask Follow-up Questions

One of the biggest advantages of Perplexity AI is that you can continue the same conversation instead of starting again.

Example:
  • Question 1: Explain SEO.
  • Question 2: Give practical SEO examples.
  • Question 3: Recommend free SEO tools.
  • Question 4: Create a 30-day SEO learning plan.

🌐 Check the Sources

Always review the source links shown with the answer. Reading the original source helps you understand the topic better and verify important information.

  • ✅ Verify important facts.
  • ✅ Compare multiple sources.
  • ✅ Read official websites whenever possible.

🎓 Best Research Tips

  • Ask one clear question at a time.
  • Give enough details in your prompt.
  • Use follow-up questions instead of starting over.
  • Review the cited sources.
  • Save useful research for future reference.

💡 Pro Tip

Don't ask only "SEO". Ask: Create a beginner-friendly SEO roadmap with free learning resources, practical tasks and recommended AI tools. Detailed prompts always produce better research results.

🎯 Lesson Summary

You have learned how to search effectively, ask better questions, continue conversations, verify information through sources and perform professional research using Perplexity AI.

Lesson 4

🎯 Prompt Engineering for Perplexity AI

The quality of your answer depends on the quality of your prompt. A prompt is simply the instruction or question you give to Perplexity AI. The more detailed your prompt is, the more accurate and useful the response will be.

❌ Weak Prompt

SEO

This prompt is too short. Perplexity AI has very little information about what you actually need.

✅ Strong Prompt

Explain SEO for beginners with practical examples, recommended free tools and a 30-day learning roadmap.

This prompt is detailed, so the AI can provide a much better answer.

📚 Research Prompts

  • Explain Artificial Intelligence with real-world examples.
  • Compare ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity AI.
  • Summarize the latest AI trends.
  • Create a research report about digital marketing.
  • Explain blockchain technology in simple words.

💼 Freelancing Prompts

  • Best freelancing skills for beginners.
  • Create a Fiverr profile description.
  • Write an Upwork proposal for a web developer.
  • Suggest high-income online skills.
  • How can I get my first freelance client?

📝 Content Writing Prompts

  • Write a professional blog about AI.
  • Create a YouTube video script.
  • Generate Instagram captions.
  • Write an SEO-friendly article.
  • Create an email newsletter.

💻 Coding Prompts

  • Create a responsive HTML landing page.
  • Write a JavaScript calculator.
  • Fix errors in my CSS code.
  • Explain Python loops with examples.
  • Create a modern login page.

💡 Formula for the Best Prompt

Use this simple formula:

Task + Details + Goal + Output Format

Example: Create a professional blog about Artificial Intelligence for beginners. Use simple English, include headings, practical examples and a conclusion.

🚀 Pro Tip

Never ask only one-word questions. Instead of: ❌ AI Ask: ✅ Explain Artificial Intelligence for beginners with examples, advantages, disadvantages and future career opportunities.

🎯 Lesson Summary

You learned how to write effective prompts for research, freelancing, content writing and coding. Better prompts produce better AI answers and save valuable time.

Lesson 5

📖 Deep Research & Source Verification

One of the biggest strengths of Perplexity AI is its ability to perform deep research while providing references to the sources used. Instead of reading dozens of websites yourself, Perplexity collects important information, summarizes it and presents it in a clear and organized way.

🔍 What is Deep Research?

Deep Research means collecting information from multiple trusted sources, analyzing it and presenting the most useful findings in one place. This helps you save time while improving the quality of your research.

📚 Why Source Verification Matters

AI is a powerful assistant, but important information should always be verified. Perplexity AI includes source references so you can easily check where the information came from.

  • ✅ Verify important facts.
  • ✅ Read the original source.
  • ✅ Compare information from different websites.
  • ✅ Use trusted sources for assignments and reports.

🎓 Academic Research

Students can use Perplexity AI to understand complex topics, summarize articles and collect information for assignments. Instead of copying answers, use AI to understand the subject and write in your own words.

Example Prompt:

Explain Climate Change with recent research, major causes, effects and possible solutions. Include trusted sources.

💼 Business Research

Business owners and freelancers can quickly research competitors, market trends and customer needs.

Example Prompt:

Analyze the latest digital marketing trends for small businesses and recommend the best strategies for 2026.

📊 Compare Multiple Sources

Instead of trusting one website, compare information from several reliable sources before making decisions.

  • Official Websites
  • Educational Institutions
  • Research Organizations
  • Industry Experts

📄 Generate Research Reports

Perplexity AI can help organize research into a structured report.

Suggested Structure:
  • Introduction
  • Main Findings
  • Supporting Evidence
  • Conclusion
  • References

💡 Pro Tip

Don't ask: ❌ Tell me about AI.

Ask instead: ✅ Create a detailed research report about Artificial Intelligence, including current trends, real-world applications, advantages, challenges and trusted sources.

🎯 Lesson Summary

In this lesson, you learned how to perform deep research, verify information using trusted sources, compare multiple references and create professional research reports using Perplexity AI.
