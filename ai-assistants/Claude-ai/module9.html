  2. Research the audience.

  4. Write the first draft.
  5. Edit and improve readability.
  6. Optimize for SEO.

SEO Content Strategy with Claude AI

Search Engine Optimization (SEO) helps your content rank higher in search engines. Claude AI can assist in creating well-structured, keyword-focused content that provides value to readers.

SEO Content Checklist

  • Research target keywords.
  • Create engaging titles.
  • Use proper heading structure (H1-H3).
  • Write valuable and original content.
  • Add FAQs and conclusion.
  • Improve readability.
  • Optimize meta title and description.

Professional SEO Prompt

"Act as an SEO specialist. Create a complete SEO article about Claude AI for beginners including title, meta description, headings, FAQs and a conclusion."
