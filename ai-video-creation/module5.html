AI Voice & Narration

Introduction

A great AI video needs a professional voice. AI voice generators can create natural-sounding narration in multiple languages and accents without recording your own voice.

What is AI Voice Generation?

AI Voice Generation converts written text into realistic speech using Artificial Intelligence. It is also called Text-to-Speech (TTS).

Voice Cloning

Voice Cloning allows AI to create a digital version of a person's voice. After training on sample audio, AI can read new scripts in a similar voice.

Background Music

Background music creates emotion and keeps viewers engaged. Choose music that matches the mood of your video without overpowering the narration.

Sound Effects

  • Nature Sounds
  • City Sounds
  • Rain
  • Explosion
  • Footsteps
  • Keyboard Clicks
  • Whoosh Effects

AI Lip Sync

Lip Sync technology automatically matches mouth movements with AI-generated speech, making talking characters look more realistic.

Example Workflow

Write a script → Generate AI Voice → Add Background Music → Add Sound Effects → Sync Voice with Video → Export Final Video

Practice Exercise

Write a one-minute script about Artificial Intelligence and generate a natural AI voice for it. Add suitable background music and export the final video.
