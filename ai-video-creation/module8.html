Module 8

Marketing Videos with AI

Introduction

Marketing videos help businesses promote products, services, and brands. AI allows you to create professional advertisements in a fraction of the time required by traditional editing.

Types of Marketing Videos

  • Product Advertisements
  • Business Promotions
  • Brand Story Videos
  • Explainer Videos
  • Social Media Ads
  • Customer Testimonial Videos

Product Advertisement

Highlight the product's main features, benefits, and unique selling points. Use attractive visuals, smooth transitions, and a clear call to action.

Brand Promotion

  • Introduce the brand.
  • Show company values.
  • Build customer trust.
  • Create a memorable identity.

Explainer Videos

Explain how a product or service works using simple animations, AI voiceovers, subtitles, and step-by-step demonstrations.

AI Sales Video Formula

Hook → Problem → Solution → Benefits → Social Proof → Call to Action

Best Practices

  • Keep videos short and engaging.
  • Use high-quality visuals.
  • Add subtitles for better accessibility.
  • Maintain consistent branding.
  • End with a strong call to action.

Practice Project

Create a 30-second AI advertisement for a smartwatch. Include a product showcase, AI voiceover, background music, and an ending call to action.

Assignment

  • Create one product advertisement.
  • Create one business promotion video.
  • Create one explainer video.
  • Export all videos in Full HD.
Pro Tip: Focus on the customer's problem first, then show how your product or service solves it. This makes marketing videos much more effective.
