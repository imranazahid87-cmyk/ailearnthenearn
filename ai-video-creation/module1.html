Introduction to AI Video Creation

1. What is AI Video Creation?

AI Video Creation means producing videos with Artificial Intelligence instead of creating everything manually. You simply provide a text prompt or image, and AI generates scenes, motion, characters, voices, subtitles, music, and effects automatically.

2. Why Learn AI Video Creation?

  • Create videos in minutes.
  • No professional editing skills required.
  • Build YouTube Automation channels.
  • Create Instagram Reels & TikTok videos.
  • Sell AI video services on Fiverr & Upwork.
  • Start your own AI content business.

3. AI Video Workflow

  1. Choose a video idea.
  2. Write a detailed prompt.
  3. Generate the video using AI.
  4. Edit the video.
  5. Add AI voice and subtitles.
  6. Export and publish.

4. Text-to-Video vs Image-to-Video

Text-to-Video: AI creates a video directly from your written prompt.

Image-to-Video: AI animates an existing image into a moving video.
