Module 7

YouTube & Social Media Videos

Introduction

Social media is one of the biggest opportunities for AI creators. You can produce engaging videos for YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and other platforms using AI tools.

YouTube Automation

  • Choose a profitable niche.
  • Write AI-generated scripts.
  • Create AI voiceovers.
  • Generate AI videos.
  • Edit and publish consistently.

YouTube Shorts

  • Vertical 9:16 format.
  • Length under 60 seconds.
  • Strong hook in the first 3 seconds.
  • Fast-paced editing.
  • Clear captions.

Instagram Reels

  • Use trending audio.
  • Create visually attractive videos.
  • Add captions.
  • Include a call to action.

TikTok Videos

  • Short storytelling.
  • Fast transitions.
  • Trending topics.
  • Creative AI effects.

Facebook Videos

  • Educational content.
  • Business promotions.
  • Product demonstrations.
  • Community engagement.

Thumbnail Design Tips

  • Large readable text.
  • High contrast colors.
  • Clear subject.
  • Simple design.
  • Consistent branding.

Video SEO Basics

  • Use keywords in the title.
  • Write a detailed description.
  • Add relevant hashtags.
  • Create an attractive thumbnail.
  • Use accurate tags.

Publishing Checklist

✔ HD Quality
✔ Correct Thumbnail
✔ Good Title
✔ Description
✔ Hashtags
✔ Captions
✔ Call to Action

Practice Project

Create a 30-second AI-generated YouTube Shorts video on any educational topic. Add subtitles, background music, and a custom thumbnail.

Assignment

  • Create one YouTube Shorts video.
  • Create one Instagram Reel.
  • Design a thumbnail.
  • Write an SEO-friendly title and description.
Pro Tip: Consistency matters more than perfection. Publishing high-quality videos regularly is one of the best ways to grow your audience.
