Module 2

Writing Professional AI Video Prompts

What is a Prompt?

A prompt is the instruction you give to an AI model. The better your prompt, the better your AI video will be.

Basic Prompt Structure

  • Subject
  • Action
  • Location
  • Camera Angle
  • Lighting
  • Video Style
  • Quality

Camera Angles

  • Close-up
  • Wide Shot
  • Drone View
  • Top View
  • Eye Level
  • Cinematic Tracking Shot

Lighting Styles

  • Golden Hour
  • Sunset
  • Neon Light
  • Studio Lighting
  • Soft Light
  • Night Lighting

Character Description

Always describe your character clearly. Mention:

  • Age
  • Gender
  • Clothing
  • Facial Expression
  • Action
  • Environment

Professional Prompt Formula

Subject + Action + Background + Camera + Lighting + Style + Quality

Example Prompt

A confident businessman walking through a futuristic city, cinematic tracking shot, golden hour lighting, ultra realistic, 4K, highly detailed.

Practice

Create a 10-second prompt showing a luxury sports car driving through Dubai at night with neon lights and cinematic camera movement.

Assignment

  • Create a travel prompt.
  • Create a food commercial prompt.
  • Create a motivational video prompt.
