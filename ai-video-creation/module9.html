Freelancing with AI Video Creation

Introduction

AI Video Creation is a high-demand freelance skill. Businesses, YouTubers, startups, agencies, and content creators regularly hire freelancers to produce professional AI-generated videos.

Best Freelancing Platforms

  • Fiverr
  • Upwork
  • Freelancer
  • PeoplePerHour
  • LinkedIn
  • Direct Clients

Create a Strong Portfolio

  • Product Advertisement
  • Real Estate Video
  • YouTube Intro
  • Travel Video
  • Social Media Ad
  • Business Explainer Video

Writing Winning Proposals

Introduce yourself briefly.

Explain how you can solve the client's problem.

Show similar work from your portfolio.

Mention the delivery time.

End with a professional call to action.

Pricing Strategy

  • Basic Package
  • Standard Package
  • Premium Package
  • Offer Add-on Services
  • Charge More as Experience Grows

Communicating with Clients

  • Reply quickly.
  • Ask clear questions.
  • Confirm project requirements.
  • Provide progress updates.
  • Deliver work on time.

Project Delivery Checklist

  • Final MP4 Video
  • HD or 4K Export
  • Correct Aspect Ratio
  • Background Music
  • Subtitles (if required)
  • Thumbnail (optional)

Practice Project

Create a complete AI promotional video for a coffee shop. Prepare a portfolio page and write a proposal as if you are sending it to a real client.

Assignment

  • Create a Fiverr Gig.
  • Design three portfolio projects.
  • Write two client proposals.
  • Create Basic, Standard, and Premium packages.
Pro Tip: Deliver high-quality work before the deadline and communicate professionally. Happy clients often return with more projects and recommend your services to others.
