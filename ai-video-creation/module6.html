AI Video Editing

Introduction to AI Video Editing

AI Video Editing allows you to edit videos automatically. AI can cut unwanted clips, improve quality, add subtitles, remove backgrounds, and apply effects within minutes.

Essential Editing Workflow

  1. Import your video.
  2. Trim unnecessary parts.
  3. Add AI captions.
  4. Apply transitions.
  5. Improve colors.
  6. Add music and sound effects.
  7. Export the final video.

AI Editing Features

  • Auto Trim
  • Auto Cut Silence
  • Auto Captions
  • Background Removal
  • Object Removal
  • Noise Reduction
  • Face Enhancement
  • AI Upscaling

Captions & Subtitles

Captions improve viewer engagement and make videos easier to understand. AI can automatically generate subtitles in multiple languages.

Color Correction

  • Brightness
  • Contrast
  • Saturation
  • White Balance
  • HDR Enhancement

Transitions & Effects

  • Fade In
  • Fade Out
  • Zoom
  • Slide
  • Blur Transition
  • Cinematic Effects

Export Settings

  • Resolution: 1920×1080 (Full HD)
  • Frame Rate: 30 FPS or 60 FPS
  • Format: MP4
  • Codec: H.264
  • Aspect Ratio: 16:9 or 9:16

Practice Project

Edit a 30-second AI-generated video by trimming clips, adding subtitles, background music, transitions, and exporting it in Full HD.

Assignment

  • Edit a travel video.
  • Create subtitles in English.
  • Add cinematic transitions.
  • Export the final MP4 video.
Pro Tip: Keep your edits simple and smooth. Clean cuts, readable captions, and balanced audio often look more professional than using too many effects.
