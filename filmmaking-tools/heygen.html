01

HeyGen kya hai?

FOUNDATION

HeyGen ek AI video platform hai jo script ko ek realistic "talking avatar" video mein badal deta hai. Aap ek script paste karte ho, ek avatar chunte ho (ready-made, apni photo se banaya hua, ya apna khud ka digital twin), voice select karte ho — aur HeyGen baaki sab kar deta hai: lip-sync, expressions, hand gestures, sab kuch.

Iska sabse bada use case hai — bina camera ke professional video banana. Teachers, marketers, aur content creators isse training videos, ads, aur explainers bina shoot kiye bana rahe hain.

Samajh lo: HeyGen editing tool nahi hai — yeh "video generation" tool hai. Aap script likhte ho, woh video render karta hai.
02

Account setup aur interface tour

GETTING STARTED

Sign up karne ke baad aapko dashboard milega jahan HeyGen ke saare tools ek jagah dikhte hain:

  • Video Avatar — apna avatar ek baar record karo, phir baar-baar reuse karo.
  • Photo Avatar / Avatar IV — ek still photo ko bolne wale video mein badlo.
  • Video Agent — sirf ek prompt se poora structured video generate karo.
  • Video Translator — existing video ko dusri language mein lip-sync ke saath translate karo.
  • AI Studio — scene-by-scene editor, jahan final touches diye jaate hain.

Shuru mein free plan try karo — usme limited credits milte hain jisse aap workflow samajh sako bina paisa laga.

03

Apna avatar banana

CORE SKILL

Do tarike hain avatar banane ke:

  • Photo Avatar: Ek clean, centered headshot upload karo — achhi lighting, neutral background. HeyGen usse ek short talking clip mein convert kar deta hai. Ek 60-second clip aam taur par 2–3 minute mein render ho jaata hai.
  • Video Avatar (Digital Twin): Ek chhoti si recording (kuch seconds se lekar 15 second tak) do, aur HeyGen ek full-motion avatar banata hai jise aap baar-baar naye scripts ke saath use kar sakte ho.
Tip: Photo jitni "professional portrait" jaisi hogi — even lighting, clear face, koi complex background nahi — result utna hi clean aayega. Side-angle ya messy background wali photos mein edges par artifacts dikh sakte hain.

Agar real face use nahi karna chahte, toh ek fully AI-generated character bhi bana sakte ho — age, gender, appearance sab customize karke. Yeh faceless ya branded character content ke liye kaam aata hai.

04

Script se video: Video Agent

MOST POWERFUL

Video Agent HeyGen ka sabse ambitious feature hai — ek text prompt do, aur AI poora structured video plan karke bana deta hai: scenes, script, avatar placement, sab kuch.

Agar khud likhna chahte ho, toh AI Script Generator use karo jo content pehle se draft kar deta hai. Recording karte waqt teleprompter camera ke saath saath chalta hai, aur AI Eye Contact Correction us halke neeche dekhne wale gaze ko fix kar deta hai jo teleprompter padhte waqt aata hai.

05

Voice cloning aur translation

SCALE

HeyGen ka Video Translator ek existing video le kar usse 100+ languages mein translate kar sakta hai — aur lip movement ko bhi naye audio ke saath sync kar deta hai, taaki avatar us language mein hi bolta hua lage.

Voice library se ready voices chuno, ya apni khud ki voice clone karke use karo — taaki har video mein aapki hi awaaz sune.

06

AI Studio mein final editing

POLISH

AI Studio ek scene-by-scene editor hai — yahan aap generate hue video ke individual clips ko refine kar sakte ho: timing adjust karna, avatar ka motion style (jaise expressive ya emotive preset) badalna, aur scenes ko reorder karna.

Ek naya feature, HyperFrames, ek interesting trick karta hai — yeh plain HTML/webpage ko seedha video mein render kar deta hai, jisse web content aur video production ek hi workflow mein aa jaate hain.

07

Beginner ke liye pro tips

SHORTCUTS
  • Photo upload karte waqt — recent photo, high resolution, aur ek se zyada expressions try karo, HeyGen khud batata hai upload screen par kya chahiye.
  • Lambi video banani ho toh chhote scenes mein todo — editing aur retry karna aasan ho jaata hai.
  • Script mein emotional cues likho ("excited tone mein") — avatar us hisaab se micro-expressions aur gestures adjust karta hai.
  • Pehla draft hamesha AI Studio mein review karo before final export — chhoti timing glitches yahin pakdi jaati hain.
08

Pricing samajhna

DECISION

HeyGen ka free plan low-friction hai — quick test videos ke liye kaafi hai. Paid plans usage aur avatar/voice cloning jaisi advanced features unlock karte hain, aur ek separate developer API bhi available hai bade scale ke liye.

Suggestion: Pehle free plan par poora workflow (avatar → script → export) ek baar chalao. Agar output aapke content style ke liye kaam karta hai, tabhi upgrade karo.
