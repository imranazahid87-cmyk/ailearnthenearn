01 · Intro

Synthesia hai kya cheez?

Synthesia ek AI avatar video platform hai — tum sirf ek script likhte ho, aur AI presenter usse bolte hue ek professional video bana deta hai. Na camera chahiye, na studio.

2017 se London mein bana yeh platform khaas kar enterprise training, onboarding aur internal communication videos ke liye jaana jaata hai. Iske avatars ki lip-sync accuracy aur micro-expressions category mein sabse natural mane jaate hain.

05 · Kiske liye

Kab use karein, kab nahi

Synthesia sabse zyada kaam ka hai jab video script-driven ho aur output predictable ho.

training videos onboarding multilingual comms L&D content

Agar tumhe fast, casual, credit-based iteration chahiye toh HeyGen jaisa tool better fit ho sakta hai. Aur yaad rakho — Synthesia ka koi dedicated mobile app nahi hai, editor sirf desktop browser ke liye bana hai.
