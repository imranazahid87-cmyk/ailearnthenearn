Adobe Podcast (Enhance)

Kharab recording ko studio-quality audio mein badalna, bilkul free.

Adobe Podcast Enhance Hai Kya?

Adobe Podcast (pehle "Project Shasta" naam se jaana jaata tha) ek free, browser-based AI tool hai jo kisi bhi voice recording ko clean, studio-quality audio mein badal deta hai — background noise, echo, aur room reverb hata kar.

Sabse khaas feature: Mic Upgrade

Agar aapne phone ya laptop ke saadhe mic se record kiya hai, to Adobe Podcast us awaaz ko AI se aise process karta hai jaise wo ek professional studio microphone se record hui ho.

Key Features

  • Background noise aur echo automatically remove
  • Sirf voice ko isolate karta hai, baaki sab mute
  • Poori tarah browser mein chalta hai — koi software install nahi
  • MP3, WAV, MP4 jaise common formats support
Kis ke liye best: Podcasters, YouTubers, aur kisi bhi jo noisy room ya remote interview ki audio clean karna chahta hai.

Step-by-Step: Kaise Use Karein

  1. podcast.adobe.com par jaayein aur free Adobe account se sign in karein.
  2. "Enhance Speech" tool kholein.
  3. Apni audio ya video file upload karein.
  4. AI processing complete hone ka wait karein (file length ke hisaab se kuch seconds se minutes tak).
  5. "Before" aur "After" version ko sunkar compare karein.
  6. Result se khush hain to enhanced file download kar lein.
Tip: Apni original raw recording hamesha safe rakhein — enhance karne ke baad agar result pasand na aaye to dobara try kar sakte hain.

Pro Tips & Real Use Cases

  • Ye tool sirf voice-only content ke liye best kaam karta hai — agar audio mein music bhi mixed hai to result utna clean nahi hoga.
  • Zoom/Google Meet interviews record karke unki audio quality improve karne ke liye perfect.
  • Free tier ki kuch limits (length/monthly usage) hoti hain — current limits Adobe ki site par check kar lein.
  • Enhance karne ke baad file ko further editing (cuts, filler-word removal) ke liye Descript mein le jaana ek common workflow hai.
Real use case: Ek podcaster jo apne phone se guest interview record karta hai, use Adobe Podcast se enhance karke phir Descript mein final edit karta hai.
