Lovo AI

Text se voiceover aur poora marketing video — Genny studio ke saath.

Lovo AI Hai Kya?

Lovo AI ek text-to-speech aur AI video creation platform hai. Iska studio tool "Genny" voiceover aur video creation ko ek hi jagah combine karta hai.

Key Features

  • 500+ voices, 100+ languages/accents mein (claim ke mutabiq)
  • Emotion tags — voice ko "excited", "calm", "serious" jaisa tone dena
  • Video templates ke saath voiceover directly combine karna
  • Pronunciation aur pause manual control (SSML jaisa)
Kis ke liye best: E-learning creators, marketing teams, aur corporate training video banane waale.

Step-by-Step: Kaise Use Karein

  1. lovo.ai par account banayein.
  2. Genny Studio kholein, naya project start karein.
  3. Apna script likhein ya paste karein.
  4. Voice, language, aur accent choose karein.
  5. Pause, emphasis, aur pronunciation manually adjust karein.
  6. Agar chahiye to background music ya video template add karein.
  7. Final voiceover/video export karein.

Pro Tips & Real Use Cases

  • Emotion tags use karke voice ko zyada natural aur engaging banayein.
  • E-learning course ke poore series mein same voice use karein — isse learner ke liye consistency banti hai.
  • Free trial limited hota hai; regular use ke liye subscription lena padega.
Real use case: Ek HR team apni company ke naye employees ke liye onboarding training video, Genny se voiceover ke saath khud bana leti hai — bina studio hire kiye.
