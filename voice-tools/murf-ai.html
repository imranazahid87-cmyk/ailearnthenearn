Murf AI

Presentations aur e-learning ke liye professional voiceover — timing ke saath perfect sync.

Murf AI Hai Kya?

Murf AI ek text-to-speech / voiceover generation tool hai jo khaas taur par business use — presentations, training videos, aur marketing — ke liye banaya gaya hai.

Key Features

  • 120+ voices, kai languages aur accents mein
  • Timeline-style editor — voice ko video ya slides ke timing se sync karna
  • Voice Changer — apni khud ki recorded voice ko AI voice mein convert karna
  • Team collaboration features (agencies ke liye useful)
Kis ke liye best: Corporate teams, sales presentations, aur product demo voiceovers banane waale.

Step-by-Step: Kaise Use Karein

  1. murf.ai par account banayein.
  2. Naya project banayein — template ya blank canvas choose karein.
  3. Script likhein aur voice select karein.
  4. Timeline mein pause, pitch, aur speed adjust karein.
  5. Agar video ya slides hain, unhe timeline mein import karke voice se sync karein.
  6. Preview sunkar final changes karein.
  7. Audio ya video export karein.

Pro Tips & Real Use Cases

  • Pronunciation dictionary use karke brand names ya technical terms sahi tarah bulwayein.
  • Slides ke saath voice ko exactly sync karne ke liye timeline editor use karein — isse video professional lagta hai.
  • Free plan mein limited minutes per month milte hain.
Real use case: Ek sales team apni product demo PowerPoint ko Murf se voiceover deke self-explanatory video bana leti hai, jo clients ko email kiya ja sakta hai.
