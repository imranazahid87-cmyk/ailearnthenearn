Suno AI

Sirf text prompt se poora gaana banao — lyrics, melody, aur vocals sab AI se.

Suno AI Hai Kya?

Suno AI text prompts se poore original songs generate karta hai — lyrics, music, aur AI vocals sab kuch, ek hi request mein.

Key Features

  • Genre, mood, aur style describe karke unique tracks banana (jaise "upbeat pop song about friendship")
  • Apne khud ke lyrics paste karke, AI unpar melody aur singing add kar sakta hai
  • "Extend" feature se generated song ko lamba karna
  • Free daily/monthly credits milte hain
Kis ke liye best: Content creators (intro/theme music), songwriters (quick ideas), aur hobbyists.

Step-by-Step: Kaise Use Karein

  1. suno.com par account banayein.
  2. "Create" section mein apna song prompt likhein (genre + mood + topic).
  3. Ya "Custom Mode" mein khud lyrics likhein aur style tag dein.
  4. Generate dabayein — Suno usually 2 variations banata hai.
  5. Dono sunkar jo pasand aaye wo save/download karein.
  6. Chahen to "Extend" feature se song ko lamba karein.

Pro Tips & Real Use Cases

  • Bahut specific prompts (instruments, tempo, era — jaise "80s synth pop") behtar result dete hain.
  • Commercial use se pehle current plan ke terms zaroor check karein — free tier ka output kabhi non-commercial hota hai.
  • YouTubers apna intro/outro music yahan se generate kar sakte hain, bina copyright strike ke risk ke (agar plan commercial rights deta ho).
Yaad rahe: AI-generated music ka copyright status abhi bhi evolve ho raha hai — use se pehle platform ki current terms zaroor padhein.
