01

Google Flow kya hai

Intro

Flow ek AI-powered filmmaking aur creative studio hai jise Google ne 20 May 2025 ko Google I/O mein launch kiya tha.

Yeh sirf ek "video generator" nahi hai — yeh text descriptions aur images ko cinematic video clips mein badalne wala poora creative workspace hai. Iske peeche teen Google AI models kaam karte hain: Veo (video), Imagen (images), aur Gemini (natural language prompting). Flow, Google ke pehle prototype VideoFX ka improved version hai — better visuals aur zyada natural storytelling ke saath.

Simple shabdon mein: aap plain Hindi/English mein apni scene describe karte ho — jaise "raat ko baarish mein ek akela figure chhatri ke saath chal raha hai" — aur Flow usse ek chhota cinematic clip bana deta hai, saath mein matching audio bhi.
02

03

Andar ka AI stack

Engine

Flow khud ek model nahi hai — yeh teen models ko ek production workspace mein jodta hai.

Veo 3.1

Physics aur realism mein strong, high prompt-adherence ke saath cinematic clips banata hai — ab native audio (dialogue, ambient sound) ke saath.

Imagen 4 (Nano Banana)

Plain language se still images banata hai — koi special syntax ya weighting symbols ki zaroorat nahi.

Gemini

Prompting ko intuitive banata hai — aap apna vision rozmarra ki bhasha mein describe karte ho, Gemini use samajh kar models tak pahunchata hai.

04

Core features

Toolkit

Flow ka design real filmmaking problems solve karne ke liye hua hai — jaise scenes ke beech character ka chehra badal jaana, ya galat lighting.

SceneBuilder

Har generation ko naye se shuru karne ke bajaye, ek existing shot ko extend ya continue karta hai — production mein continuity banaye rakhne ke liye sabse practical feature.

Ingredients

Prompts, characters aur reusable creative parts ko organize karne ki jagah — jab ek hi subject ke multiple variants chahiye ho.

Camera controls

Scene ke andar camera ka angle ya view badalne ki freedom — ek real camera director jaisa control.

Insert / Remove

Kisi image ya video mein object/character add karo (lighting-shadow auto-match hoga) ya kisi element ko hata do — background khud-ba-khud convincingly fill ho jata hai.

Lasso Tool

Image ke kisi bhi area ko freehand select karke, plain language mein type karo ki wahan kya badalna hai.

Flow TV

Learning layer — working examples, prompts aur techniques dikhata hai taaki aap zero se shuru karne ke bajaye real outputs se seekh sako.

05

Plans — Pro vs Ultra

Pricing

Flow free Labs experiment nahi hai — yeh Google AI ke subscription plans ke saath tied hai.

Entry
Google AI Pro
  • Flow ke key features milte hain
  • Har mahine 100 generations ki capped limit
  • Roz ke creative use ke liye theek
Higher tier
Google AI Ultra
  • Sabse zyada usage limits
  • 1080p generation
  • Advanced camera controls
  • Naye Veo features tak early access, jaise native audio wala Veo 3
Yaad rakhein: yeh product story "free Labs experiment" nahi, balki "subscription-based creation workspace" hai jo Google ke video stack ke upar tayyar kiya gaya hai.

07

Achhi prompting kaise karein

Practice
1

Plain bhasha mein likhein — koi special syntax ya weighting symbols ki zaroorat nahi, Gemini layer aapki language samajh leta hai.

2

Continuity ke liye SceneBuilder use karein — har baar naya generation banane ke bajaye pichhle shot ko extend karein, taaki character aur environment consistent rahe.

3

Ingredients mein reusable parts save karein — ek hi character ya scene ke multiple variants banane ho toh yeh time bachata hai.

4

Audio detail bhi describe karein — Veo 3.1 video ke saath dialogue aur ambient sound bhi generate karta hai, toh mood ke mutabik sound bhi mention karein.

5

Flow TV se seekhein — working prompts aur examples dekh kar samjhein ki achhe outputs kaise likhe jaate hain.

08

Kab use karein

Verdict

Google Flow sabse zyada suited hai cinematic storytelling, creative filmmaking, aur single high-quality productions ke liye — jaise short films, art projects ya ek polished hero video.

Agar aapko ek saath 10+ videos, bulk commercial content, branded ad campaigns ya multilingual output chahiye, toh us tarah ke workflow ke liye dusre bulk-focused tools zyada suited hote hain.

One-line takeaway: Flow ek director ka tool hai, ek assembly line ka nahi — quality aur craft ke liye banaya gaya hai, volume ke liye nahi.
