Kling AI hai kya cheez?

Kling, Kuaishou (Kwai wali company) ka bana hua text-to-video aur image-to-video generator hai. Simple bhasha mein: tum likhte ho ya photo dete ho, aur woh usse ek chalti-phirti video bana deta hai.

2026 mein Kling apne diffusion-transformer architecture ki wajah se khaas hai — motion realistic lagta hai, chehre consistent rehte hain, aur physics (paani, baal, kapde ka hilna) kaafi accurate dikhti hai. Yehi wajah hai ki Runway, Luma aur Google Veo ke saath iska naam seedha compare hota hai.

SCENE 04 · 00:14

Achha prompt kaise likhein

Kling camera-language samajhta hai — jaise ek cinematographer se baat kar rahe ho.

Subject

Kaun/kya hai scene mein, kya pehna hai, expression kya hai.

Action

Woh kya kar raha hai — chal raha, muskura raha, hawa mein udta hua kapda.

Camera

Close-up, slow pan, drone shot — camera movement bhi likho.

Mood

Lighting aur color feel — golden hour, neon night, soft overcast.

Yaad rakho: Vague prompt ("a girl walking") se generic result milega. Specific prompt ("a girl in a red coat walking through rain-soaked Tokyo street, neon reflections, slow tracking shot") se kaafi behtar output aata hai.

Common galtiyan aur pro tips

Zyada complex scene ek saath

Chhoti clips banao aur unhe "extend" feature se lamba karo, ek hi shot mein sab kuch mat daalo.

Hands aur fingers

Yeh AI video ki purani kamzori abhi bhi hai — close-up hand shots avoid karo.

Long consistency

Multi-clip mein character same rakhne ke liye reference image ya same seed use karo.

Credits bachana

Draft low model par, final render high-quality model par karo.
