01 — Shuru se

Pika AI hai kya cheez?

Pika ek text-to-video aur image-to-video tool hai jo pika.art par milta hai. Yeh Kling ya Veo jaise cinematic-realism wale tools se thoda alag hai — Pika ka pura focus hai creative control aur maze wale effects par.

2026 mein Pika ka Pika 2.5 engine physics-aware hai — matlab ek ball gire toh sahi weight ke saath girega, balloon fule toh sahi tarah se phoolega. Chhote (3-10 second) clips ke liye yeh best hai — Reels, TikTok, Shorts jaisa short-form content banane mein.

04 — Prompting

Achha prompt kaise likhein

Pika camera-syntax aur negative prompts dono samajhta hai.

Camera syntax

"-camera zoom in", "-camera pan left", "-camera rotate cw" jaise commands use karo.

Negative prompt

Jo nahi chahiye woh likho — "-neg distorted faces, extra limbs, text".

Lighting keywords

"Volumetric lighting", "Rembrandt lighting", "God rays" — mood turant badal jaata hai.

Yaad rakho: Effect ka naam clearly likho (jaise "explode into confetti") — Pika named effects ko achhe se pehchanta hai.
