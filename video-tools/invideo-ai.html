01 · Intro

InVideo AI hai kya cheez?

InVideo AI ek prompt-first video generator hai — tum ek idea likhte ho, aur AI khud script, voiceover, footage, subtitles, music aur transitions daal ke poori video bana deta hai.

2026 mein iski sabse badi khaasiyat yeh hai ki yeh apne pipeline ke andar OpenAI ke Sora 2 aur Google ke Veo 3.1 dono ko bundle karta hai — matlab cinematic, physics-accurate clips bhi stock footage ke saath mix ho sakte hain, sab ek hi subscription mein. Note: InVideo do products chalata hai — "InVideo AI" (prompt-first, generative) aur "InVideo Studio" (traditional drag-and-drop timeline editor) — yeh course AI product par focus karta hai.

02 · Workflow

Video kaise banti hai

  1. 01
    Prompt likhoKya video chahiye, kis tone mein, kiske liye — sab ek jagah describe karo.
  2. 02
    AI script banata haiTumhare prompt se ek structured script generate hoti hai.
  3. 03
    Voice aur visuals jud jaate hainAI voiceover aur matching stock ya generative footage khud select karta hai.
  4. 04
    Draft milta haiEk edit-ready video seconds/minutes mein taiyar.
  5. 05
    Fine-tune karoScenes, text, voice ya music manually adjust karke final export lo.

04 · Tips

Credits bachane ke tips

Draft pehle, generate baad mein

Script aur structure pehle finalize karo, phir hi generative credits use karo.

Generative clips sparingly

Sora 2/Veo clips ko premium accent ki tarah use karo, poori video generative mat banao.

Monthly limits track karo

AI minutes, iStock downloads aur voice minutes alag pools hain — sabka usage dekhte raho.

05 · Kiske liye

Kab use karein

InVideo AI un logo ke liye best hai jinhe fast, prompt-first video chahiye bina script writer, editor, voice artist alag se hire kiye.

marketing videos explainer content social ads solo creators

Agar tumhe purely original, cinematic generative video chahiye toh Runway jaisa dedicated tool better ho sakta hai. Agar frame-by-frame manual control chahiye toh InVideo Studio (traditional editor) ya CapCut dekho.
