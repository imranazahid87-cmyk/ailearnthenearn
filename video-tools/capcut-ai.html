01 · Intro

CapCut AI hai kya cheez?

CapCut ek all-in-one video editor hai jisme ab AI features bhi built-in hain — script se video, auto captions, background removal, aur ek click mein full auto-edit.

2026 mein CapCut sirf editing tool nahi raha — yeh ek poora creative suite ban gaya hai jisme video, image (Seedream) aur AI video generation (Seedance) sab shaamil hain. Sabse badi baat: iska free tier kaafi generous hai, isliye beginners ke liye yeh best starting point hai.

03 · Workflow

Auto-Edit se video kaise banayein

  1. 01
    Footage upload karoApni raw clips, photos ya voice notes CapCut mein daalo.
  2. 02
    Kya chahiye woh likhoEk chhota description do — jaise "travel vlog, upbeat tone".
  3. 03
    AI analyze karegaScene recognition, speech transcription aur quality scoring se best parts chunta hai.
  4. 04
    Draft milegaAuto color correction, audio leveling aur transitions ke saath ek pehla version ready.
  5. 05
    Khud review karoStory, timing, captions aur crop manually check karke final touch do.
Tip: AI daraft ko replacement nahi, starting point maano — taste aur timing abhi bhi tumhara kaam hai.
