01 // Intro

Descript hai kya cheez?

Descript ek AI-powered audio-video editor hai jo tumhari recording ko automatically transcribe karta hai — phir tum text edit karte ho, aur video/audio khud usi hisaab se cut ho jaata hai.

Yeh un logo ke liye perfect hai jo talking-head videos, podcasts, interviews ya tutorials banate hain. "Um", "you know" jaise filler words select karke delete karo — corresponding audio bhi turant hat jaata hai.

02 // Features

Sabse kaam ke AI tools

Overdub

Sirf kuch minute ki apni voice se digital voice-twin bana lo, missed words fix karne ke liye.

Eye Contact

Script padhte waqt camera se nazrein hati ho toh AI unhe camera ki taraf adjust kar deta hai.

Green Screen

Bina asli green screen ke background remove ya replace karo.

Redaction

Sensitive information ko auto-detect karke bleep/blur kar deta hai.

03 // Workflow

Pehla edit kaise karein

  1. 01
    Record ya upload karoDescript mein hi record karo, ya existing footage upload karo.
  2. 02
    Auto-transcribe hota haiKuch hi seconds mein poora transcript text ban jaata hai.
  3. 03
    Text edit karoJo bhi hatana hai, transcript se select karke delete karo.
  4. 04
    AI tools lagaoStudio Sound, Eye Contact ya Green Screen jahan zarurat ho.
  5. 05
    Export karoDirect export karo, ya XML se Premiere jaise editor mein bhejo.
Tip: "Paper Edit" workflow try karo — lambi recording ka transcript padho, best parts highlight karke naye composition mein copy karo. Ek rough cut minutes mein ban jaata hai.
