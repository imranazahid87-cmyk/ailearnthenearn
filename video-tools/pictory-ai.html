¶ 01 — Shuru se

Pictory AI hai kya cheez?

Pictory ek platform hai jo text (blog, script, article) se original video banata hai, ya existing video ko text ke through edit karta hai.

Isme 3 million+ stock clips aur 15,000+ music tracks ki library hai, saath mein AI voices jo tumhare liye narrate karte hain. Brand colors, fonts aur logo se video ko customize bhi kar sakte ho. Yeh un logo ke liye perfect hai jinke paas already likha hua content hai aur woh usse video mein badalna chahte hain — bina frame-by-frame editing seekhe.

Text-to-Video

Blog post ya script paste karo, video khud ban jaata hai.

AI Voices

Natural-sounding narration, 29 languages mein.

Auto Captions

Video ke saath captions bhi automatically add ho jaate hain.

¶ 03 — Workflow

Pehla video kaise banayein

  1. 01
    Content daaloBlog URL, script ya article paste karo — ya scratch se likho.
  2. 02
    AI draft dekhoPictory text ko scenes mein todta hai aur matching stock visuals suggest karta hai.
  3. 03
    Scenes edit karoHar scene ka visual, text overlay ya timing manually adjust kar sakte ho.
  4. 04
    Voice aur music choose karoAI voice pick karo aur background music library se music lagao.
  5. 05
    Export karoCaption check karke apne platform (YouTube, Reels) ke hisaab se export karo.
Tip: Ek lambi blog post se ek nahi, kai chhote video variations bana sakte ho — repurposing isi tool ki sabse badi taaqat hai.

¶ 05 — Kiske liye

Kab use karein, kab nahi

Pictory sabse zyada kaam ka hai jab tumhare paas already likha hua content ho — blog, script, ya training material.

blog-to-video YouTube repurposing training content marketing clips

Agar tumhe frame-by-frame precise editing ya cinematic control chahiye, toh yeh sahi tool nahi hai — uske liye traditional editor (jaise CapCut) better rahega. Pictory ki strength speed aur automation hai, manual control nahi.

Ek article uthao, aur dekho woh video kaise banta hai.
