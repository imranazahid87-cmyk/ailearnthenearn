Lesson 1

🧠 What is Prompt Engineering?

Prompt Engineering is the process of designing clear, detailed, and well-structured instructions that help AI generate better responses. A good prompt saves time and improves accuracy.

Why is it Important?

  • ✔ Better AI Responses
  • ✔ Higher Accuracy
  • ✔ Professional Quality
  • ✔ Faster Workflow
  • ✔ Better Creativity
  • ✔ Improved Productivity

💡 Pro Tip

Lesson 2

🏗 Structure of a Perfect Prompt

A professional prompt usually contains five important elements.

  • 🎯 Goal
  • 📝 Context
  • 👤 Role
  • 📏 Rules
  • 📦 Output Format

Example

Act as an SEO Expert. Write a 1200-word article about AI Marketing. Use beginner-friendly language. Include H2 headings, FAQs and Meta Description.

Lesson 4

🎯 Zero-Shot Prompting

Zero-shot Prompting means asking ChatGPT to complete a task without providing any examples. The AI relies only on your instructions and its existing knowledge. This is the fastest prompting technique and works well for simple tasks.

📌 Example

Write a professional email requesting one day's leave from office.

✅ Best Use Cases

  • Email Writing
  • Article Writing
  • Summaries
  • Translations
  • Brainstorming
  • General Questions

📝 Practice Exercise

Write three Zero-shot prompts for: ✔ Education ✔ Marketing ✔ Coding

Lesson 5

📘 One-Shot Prompting

One-shot Prompting means giving ChatGPT one example before asking it to perform a similar task. Providing one example helps AI understand your expectations.

📌 Example

Example: Title: Benefits of AI in Education Introduction: Artificial Intelligence is transforming education... Now write a similar introduction for: Benefits of AI in Healthcare.

🎯 Advantages

  • More Accurate Output
  • Consistent Writing Style
  • Better Formatting
  • Improved Understanding

📝 Practice

Create one writing example and ask ChatGPT to generate another in the same style.

Lesson 6

📚 Few-Shot Prompting

Few-shot Prompting means providing multiple examples before asking ChatGPT to complete a task. This technique is commonly used by professionals because it produces more consistent and high-quality results.

📌 Example

Example 1 Question: HTML stands for? Answer: HyperText Markup Language.

Example 2 Question: CSS stands for? Answer: Cascading Style Sheets.

Now Answer: Question: SEO stands for?

🌟 Benefits

  • Higher Accuracy
  • Better Formatting
  • Professional Results
  • Improved Consistency
  • Less Editing Required

💡 Pro Tip

Whenever you want ChatGPT to copy your writing style, provide two or three examples before asking for new content.

📝 Mini Challenge

Create three examples of product descriptions and ask ChatGPT to write a fourth one in the same format and tone.

Lesson 7

🧠 Chain of Thought Prompting

Chain of Thought (CoT) Prompting is an advanced prompting technique that encourages ChatGPT to solve problems step by step instead of jumping directly to the final answer. This method improves reasoning, calculations, planning, and decision-making.

📌 Example Prompt

You are a Business Consultant. Analyze this business idea step by step. Explain the advantages, disadvantages, possible risks, and finally give your recommendation.

✅ Best Uses

  • Business Planning
  • Problem Solving
  • Programming Logic
  • Research
  • Data Analysis
  • Decision Making

📝 Practice

Ask ChatGPT to create a business plan step by step for an online clothing store.

Lesson 8

📂 Professional Prompt Templates

Professional prompt templates save time and produce consistent results. Instead of writing prompts from scratch, you can reuse these templates.

📄 Universal Prompt Formula

Act as a [Role]. Help me create [Task]. The topic is [Topic]. Target audience: [Audience]. Tone: [Professional/Friendly]. Output should include [Requirements].

🎯 Example

Act as an SEO Expert. Write a 1500-word article on Artificial Intelligence. Target audience: Beginners. Include H2 headings, FAQs, Meta Title, Meta Description and Keywords.
