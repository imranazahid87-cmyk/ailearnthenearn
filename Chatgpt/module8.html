Lesson 1

🚀 Advanced Prompt Engineering

Professional users don't rely on simple prompts. They combine context, role, goals, constraints, examples, and output formatting to generate highly accurate responses. A well-designed prompt saves time and improves the overall quality of AI output.

Professional Prompt Formula

Role + Goal + Context + Rules + Output Format + Examples
