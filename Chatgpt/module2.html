Lesson 1

💬 Understanding Prompts

A prompt is the instruction you give to ChatGPT. Think of it as talking to a very intelligent assistant. The clearer your instruction, the better your answer.

Example

❌ Write an article.

✅ Write a 700-word SEO optimized article about Digital Marketing for beginners.

📝 Practice

Write three prompts for: Website Email YouTube

🧠 Giving Context

Context tells ChatGPT exactly what you need. Without context, responses may be too general.

Example

Write a blog.

Write a 1000-word beginner-friendly blog about SEO in simple English with headings.

Exercise

Improve five simple prompts by adding more details.

Lesson 4

🎭 Role Prompting

One of the most powerful prompt techniques is Role Prompting. Instead of asking a simple question, you tell ChatGPT to behave like a specific professional. This helps AI generate more accurate, focused, and high-quality responses.

📌 Examples

SEO Expert
Act as an SEO Expert and create a keyword strategy for an online clothing store.

Teacher
Act as a school teacher and explain Photosynthesis in simple English for Class 6 students.

Web Developer
Act as a professional Web Developer and create a responsive landing page using HTML and CSS.

📝 Practice Exercise

Write three Role Prompts for: ✔ Teacher ✔ Graphic Designer ✔ Marketing Expert

Lesson 5

💬 Follow-up Prompts

You don't need to start a new chat every time. Professional users continue the same conversation by asking follow-up questions. This allows ChatGPT to remember the previous context and provide better answers.

Example Conversation

👤 Write an article about SEO.

👤 Make it beginner-friendly.

👤 Add five FAQs.

👤 Make it SEO Optimized.

📝 Practice

Start one conversation and improve the response by asking at least five follow-up questions.

Lesson 6

⚠️ Common Mistakes Beginners Make

Many beginners don't get good results because they make simple mistakes. Avoiding these mistakes will immediately improve your AI experience.

  • ❌ Asking very short questions.
  • ❌ Giving no context.
  • ❌ Expecting perfect answers without editing.
  • ❌ Starting a new chat for every small task.
  • ❌ Not checking AI-generated facts.
  • ❌ Ignoring follow-up prompts.
  • ❌ Writing confusing instructions.
  • ❌ Giving multiple unrelated tasks in one prompt.

💡 Pro Tips

  • ✅ Be clear and specific.
  • ✅ Mention your goal.
  • ✅ Add examples whenever possible.
  • ✅ Break large tasks into smaller prompts.
  • ✅ Always review AI responses before publishing.
